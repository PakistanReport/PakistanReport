Opposition figures have rejected the federal government’s decision to impose emergency measures and governor’s rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, raising the prospect of constitutional litigation. The Tehreek Tahafuz Ayeen-i-Pakistan opposition alliance has announced plans to contest the measures through courts, assemblies and public forums. PTI representatives have also opposed the intervention, while lawyers differ over how presidential proclamations under Articles 232 and 234 may be reviewed by courts. Reporting on October 12 described a possible challenge before the Peshawar High Court, but a potential filing must not be presented as an already decided case. The government maintains that security and governance concerns required intervention; critics say it undermines the provincial electoral mandate.
