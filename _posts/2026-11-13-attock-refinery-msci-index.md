**KARACHI:** Attock Refinery Limited has been selected for inclusion in the MSCI Pakistan Index following the global index provider's November 2026 review, increasing the number of Pakistani companies represented in the index to 30.

The changes were announced on November 11 and will take effect after trading closes on November 30, with the revised index composition applying from December 1.

According to an analysis by Topline Securities, Attock Refinery qualified after its foreign-investor-adjusted free-float market capitalisation reached $168 million at the end of October, exceeding the minimum requirement of $116 million.

The addition comes despite a decline in Pakistan's overall weighting in the MSCI Frontier Markets Index.

Pakistan's share of the index fell to 4.98% from 5.2% in the previous review conducted in August 2026.

The reduction was attributed mainly to stronger growth in the combined market capitalisation of companies in other frontier markets, which reduced Pakistan's relative share.

The benchmark KSE-100 Index also declined 5.5% between the two review periods, according to the analysis.

Separately, MSCI removed Pakistan Oxygen Limited and Flying Cement Company Limited from its Frontier Markets Small Cap Index.

The review retained the minimum free-float market capitalisation threshold of $116 million and the total market capitalisation threshold of $232 million.

MSCI indexes are widely used by international investors to track market performance and guide investment portfolios.
