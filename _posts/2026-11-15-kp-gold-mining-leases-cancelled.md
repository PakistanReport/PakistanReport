**PESHAWAR, 15 November 2026 —** Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Governor Faisal Karim Kundi has announced the suspension of gold mining across the province and the cancellation of existing leases, citing concerns over illegal extraction, security threats and losses to public revenue.

Speaking at a press conference in Peshawar on Saturday, Kundi said mining operators and contractors had been given seven days to remove their machinery from affected sites or face confiscation.

The governor alleged that illegal mining networks were paying money to armed groups in exchange for protection, with some proceeds being used to finance militant activities.

He claimed that approximately 80 per cent of profits from certain illegal mining operations were being transferred to Afghanistan. The allegation has not been independently established.

Kundi also claimed that unauthorised mining generated around Rs4.92 billion each month and involved tens of thousands of workers and thousands of vehicles and heavy machines.

The announcement followed a deadly attack on police personnel in Kohat on 11 November. According to police, 10 officers, including Deputy Superintendent of Police Haider Zaman, were killed, while 18 militants died in the subsequent exchange of fire.

The governor announced plans to establish a task force named after Haider Zaman to prepare recommendations for regulating mining activities within three weeks.

He also said a judicial commission would investigate the Kohat attack and that action would be taken against individuals found responsible for facilitating illegal mining, regardless of their political or institutional affiliations.

Officials have linked the crackdown to efforts to protect mineral resources, improve oversight and address environmental damage associated with unauthorised extraction.

Kundi said future mining operations would be governed through a more comprehensive regulatory system, including revised arrangements for leases and worker security.

The announcement marks a major change in the province's approach to gold extraction, with existing operators facing an immediate suspension while the administration prepares new regulatory measures.
