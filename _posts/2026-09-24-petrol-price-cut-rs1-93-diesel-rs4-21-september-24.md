The government has reduced the price of petrol by Rs1.93 per litre and high-speed diesel by Rs4.21 per litre, with the revised rates taking effect on September 24.

Petrol now costs Rs390.12 per litre, down from Rs392.05, while high-speed diesel has fallen to Rs414.75 per litre from Rs418.96.

The Petroleum Division said the adjustment reflected global developments, including changes in Platts rates, premiums and other incidentals.

Fuel prices are closely watched because petrol directly affects household transport costs, while high-speed diesel is widely used in freight, agriculture and public transport and can feed into broader distribution costs.

The new rates apply from September 24 under the government's current fuel-pricing framework.
