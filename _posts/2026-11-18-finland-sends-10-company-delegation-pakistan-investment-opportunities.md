**KARACHI, 18 November 2026 —** A Finnish business delegation representing around 10 companies has visited Pakistan to explore trade and investment opportunities, with discussions focusing on energy, mining and industrial technology.

Led by Finland's Deputy Minister for International Trade, Jarno Syrjälä, the delegation met government officials and business representatives in Islamabad and Karachi.

At the Finland-Pakistan Business Summit 2026 in Karachi on Tuesday, Syrjälä called for stronger commercial cooperation. The event coincided with 75 years of diplomatic relations between the two countries.

### Energy and Industrial Cooperation

During separate meetings, Petroleum Minister Ali Pervaiz Malik highlighted opportunities in energy infrastructure and mineral exploration. Finnish companies represented in the discussions included Wärtsilä, Elematic and Casagrande Laboratories.

Adviser to the Prime Minister on Industries and Production Haroon Akhtar Khan encouraged partnerships in electric vehicles, battery manufacturing, renewable energy and advanced industrial technologies.

Wille Eerola, chairman of the Finland-Pakistan Business Council, also emphasised connecting Finnish technical expertise with Pakistani businesses.
