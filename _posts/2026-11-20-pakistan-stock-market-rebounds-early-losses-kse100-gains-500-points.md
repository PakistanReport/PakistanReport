**KARACHI, 20 November 2026 —** The Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX) recovered from early selling pressure on Friday, with the benchmark **KSE-100 Index gaining more than 500 points by midday** after initially falling by a similar margin.

At approximately noon, the index stood at **177,274.98 points**, up 502.80 points, or 0.28%, from Thursday's closing level of 176,772.18.

The recovery followed a weak opening in which the benchmark dropped 559.42 points to 176,212.76 during the first minutes of trading.

### Buying Returns to Major Sectors

Buying interest emerged in automobile assemblers, cement, oil and gas exploration, oil marketing and power generation stocks after these sectors faced selling pressure earlier in the session.

Major companies trading higher during the recovery included Hub Power Company, Oil and Gas Development Company, Pakistan Oilfields, Pakistan Petroleum, Meezan Bank and National Bank of Pakistan.

The change in direction came despite weakness in international equity markets, where Asian shares were under pressure amid concerns over the global technology sector and broader economic uncertainty.

### Market Follows Thursday's Modest Recovery

Friday's fluctuations followed a positive session on Thursday, when the KSE-100 gained **477.86 points**, or 0.27%, to close at 176,772.19.

Thursday's advance was supported by buying in fertiliser, energy and technology stocks, although profit-taking reduced the market's earlier gains.

Engro Holdings, Engro Fertilizers, Fauji Fertilizer, Mari Energies and Systems Limited were among the leading contributors to that recovery.

Analysts said domestic political developments and geopolitical uncertainty continued to influence investor sentiment, while selective buying in major companies provided support.
