**ISLAMABAD:** Pakistan Peoples Party Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari has warned that the failure of negotiations between the United States and Iran could lead to a wider regional conflict, while expressing hope that diplomatic efforts will produce a settlement.

Speaking in an interview with TRT World, the former foreign minister said the consequences of the conflict were extending beyond the Middle East, affecting economies and households worldwide.

He pointed to higher fuel and food costs, inflation and pressure on employment and economic growth as reasons for the international community to support a negotiated resolution.

Bilawal said Pakistan had played an important role in securing the initial ceasefire and maintaining dialogue between Washington and Tehran. He added that Islamabad remained actively involved in diplomatic efforts.

He expressed concern that a breakdown in negotiations could intensify the fighting and extend instability beyond the areas already affected.

The PPP chairman said he remained optimistic that both sides could reach an agreement, allowing governments to concentrate on economic recovery and other international challenges.

Discussing regional security, Bilawal welcomed growing defence cooperation among Pakistan, Saudi Arabia and Türkiye, describing it as an important development for stability.

He said he would favour expanding such cooperation to other countries, particularly members of the Gulf Cooperation Council, while maintaining that the arrangement was intended for collective defence rather than confrontation with another state.

His remarks come as uncertainty over the US–Iran diplomatic process continues to affect regional security and global economic expectations.
