The International Monetary Fund has projected that Pakistan's economy will grow by 3.5% in fiscal year 2026–27, while inflation is forecast at 9.9%, according to its October 2026 economic outlook.

Both figures are projections and are subject to revision as economic conditions and policy assumptions change.

The growth estimate points to continued economic expansion, while the inflation forecast indicates that price pressures remain a significant concern.

Actual outcomes will depend on domestic economic activity, external financing conditions, commodity prices and the implementation of economic policies.

The forecasts should not be confused with final figures for the fiscal year, which has not yet ended.
