**ISLAMABAD:** Portuguese Foreign Minister Paulo Rangel is scheduled to begin a two-day official visit to Pakistan on Sunday, November 22, aimed at strengthening diplomatic relations and expanding trade and investment cooperation between the two countries.

Rangel is visiting at the invitation of Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar, with a delegation representing approximately 20 Portuguese companies.

According to Pakistan's Foreign Office, the two foreign ministers will hold bilateral discussions, followed by delegation-level talks and a joint press briefing.

The meetings will review existing relations and explore cooperation in agriculture, particularly olive cultivation and processing, renewable energy, maritime connectivity, migration and education.

The Portuguese minister is also expected to meet President Asif Ali Zardari, Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif and other senior Pakistani officials.

A Pakistan–Portugal Business Forum is scheduled for November 23 in Islamabad, bringing together companies and investors to explore partnerships in agriculture, pharmaceuticals, information technology, renewable energy and textiles.

The Portuguese delegation will also participate in a trade and investment conference at the Islamabad Chamber of Commerce and Industry, alongside business-to-business meetings with Pakistani companies.

The visit coincides with the 77th anniversary of diplomatic relations between Pakistan and Portugal, which were established on November 7, 1949.

Both countries are seeking to strengthen economic engagement while maintaining dialogue on regional and international developments of mutual interest.
