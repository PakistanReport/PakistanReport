North Korea fired multiple ballistic missiles toward waters off its east coast on Monday, October 12, according to South Korea’s military. The [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/north-korea-kim-missiles-south-5b2d3a2d9a31118648b9f82022b9c4e3) reported that the launches added to a series of recent weapons tests amid heightened regional tensions. South Korea's Joint Chiefs of Staff said at least one missile travelled more than 900 kilometres, according to [Yonhap](https://en.yna.co.kr/view/AEN20261012002700315). Japanese authorities said at least two projectiles fell outside Japan’s exclusive economic zone and reported no damage. South Korea condemned the launches, citing United Nations Security Council restrictions on North Korea’s ballistic missile activity. The reports did not establish an attack on South Korea or Japan, and this story should not imply one. The launches are significant because they increase pressure on already strained regional security relations involving Seoul, Tokyo, Pyongyang and Washington. Technical details such as missile type and range remain subject to official assessment and should be updated when corroborated.
