Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb has reaffirmed the government's commitment to supporting exporters, small businesses and private-sector investment during a meeting with Lahore Chamber of Commerce and Industry (LCCI) President Ali Hussam Asghar.

The meeting, held on November 2, focused on export-led growth, access to finance and improving Pakistan's business environment.

## Exports and small businesses in focus

According to the Press Information Department, Aurangzeb emphasised the importance of expanding Pakistan's export base and making domestic businesses more competitive in international markets.

He highlighted the need for simpler regulations, predictable policies and better coordination between government institutions.

The discussion also covered support for small and medium enterprises (SMEs), including improved financing, business expansion and investment in modernisation.

## Tax reforms and policy stability

LCCI President Ali Hussam Asghar stressed the importance of easier access to finance, regulatory stability and continued consultation with business representatives.

Aurangzeb said tax and regulatory policies should encourage productive economic activity while improving documentation and broadening the tax base.

He also highlighted the Tax Policy Office's role in developing a medium-term tax framework to provide greater certainty for businesses.

The meeting was attended by senior finance officials, including the governor of the State Bank of Pakistan.
