**KARACHI:** Pakistan Stock Exchange Limited (PSX) reported consolidated profit after tax of Rs558.01 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, down 19.9% from Rs696.93 million in the corresponding period last year.

The exchange operator disclosed its financial results on Wednesday, October 28.

Consolidated revenue stood at Rs731.35 million, compared with Rs734.62 million a year earlier, representing a decline of approximately 0.4%.

Operating profit fell 34.2% to Rs135.43 million from Rs205.67 million, a considerably larger reduction than the movement in revenue.

Earnings per share decreased to Rs0.70 from Rs0.87 in the comparative quarter.

The company's cash and bank balances reached Rs930.15 million at September 30, compared with Rs840.14 million at the end of June 2026.

The board did not recommend an interim cash dividend, bonus shares or rights issue for the quarter.

The results reflect the financial performance of Pakistan Stock Exchange Limited as a company, rather than changes in the KSE-100 index or the overall value of listed shares.
