Pakistan’s Sensitive Price Indicator increased 11.97% year-on-year in the week ended October 8, according to Pakistan Bureau of Statistics data reported on Saturday. The index also rose 0.57% week-on-week.

Wheat flour was among the leading contributors, rising 5.69% during the week. Chicken increased 4.93% and gram pulse 3.99%, illustrating uneven price pressure across essential items.

The SPI measures changes in a selected basket of frequently purchased goods and is not the same measure as monthly Consumer Price Index inflation. The 11.97% figure represents a comparison with the corresponding week last year, not a single-week jump.
