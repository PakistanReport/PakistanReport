**ISLAMABAD, 18 November 2026 —** Petrol prices in Pakistan have been reduced by Rs3 per litre to Rs325, while the price of high-speed diesel remains unchanged at Rs358 per litre, according to a notification issued by the Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority (OGRA).

The revised prices took effect on Wednesday, following the regulator's announcement on Tuesday evening under the federal government's petroleum pricing mechanism.

The latest adjustment follows an earlier reduction of Rs2 per litre in both petrol and diesel prices on 16 November. Petrol has consequently fallen by Rs5 per litre across the two revisions, while diesel has declined by Rs2.

### Relief for petrol consumers

The latest cut offers modest relief to motorcycle riders, private motorists and other consumers who rely on petrol for daily transport.

However, diesel prices have not changed in the latest adjustment. High-speed diesel is widely used in commercial transport, agriculture and freight operations, making its price particularly important for the cost of moving goods.

### Global oil prices and domestic charges

The latest adjustment comes amid changes in international petroleum prices and import-related costs.

According to Business Recorder, international benchmark petrol prices declined during the preceding week, contributing to lower domestic pricing calculations.

Petroleum taxes and levies continue to account for a substantial portion of the retail price. The government has also introduced a temporary arrangement under which oil refineries contribute towards the petroleum levy to support consumer relief.

The latest reduction extends the recent downward trend in petrol prices, although the unchanged diesel rate limits the immediate benefit for businesses dependent on heavy transport.
