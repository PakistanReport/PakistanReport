**KARACHI, 20 November 2026 —** Pakistan received **55,417 metric tonnes of liquefied petroleum gas (LPG)** through six vessels arriving at Port Qasim between 12 and 18 November, according to shipping figures attributed to the Port Qasim Authority.

The shipments come ahead of the winter season, when demand for LPG typically increases as households and businesses rely more heavily on alternative fuels for cooking and heating.

### Two Vessels Account for Most of the Imports

The largest consignment arrived aboard *Seashine* on 18 November, carrying **21,866 tonnes**, while *Pacific Star 1* delivered another **20,115 tonnes** on 16 November.

Together, the two vessels transported approximately 76% of the total LPG received during the seven-day period.

Four smaller vessels completed the shipments: *Gas RGA* with 1,621 tonnes, *Victoria Kosan* with 4,387 tonnes, *Leonara Kosan* with 3,877 tonnes and *Alisa 1* with 3,551 tonnes.

### Imports Expected to Support Winter Supplies

The arrival of the six consignments is expected to strengthen domestic LPG availability before colder weather increases consumption.

LPG is an important alternative fuel for households and commercial users, particularly in areas where piped natural gas supplies are unavailable or insufficient.

While the additional imports could ease supply pressures, their impact on retail prices will depend on distribution, market demand and other costs.
