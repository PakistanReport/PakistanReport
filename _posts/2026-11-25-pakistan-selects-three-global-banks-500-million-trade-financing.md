**ISLAMABAD:** Pakistan has selected three international banks to arrange a $500 million trade financing facility backed by a proposed guarantee from the Multilateral Investment Guarantee Agency (MIGA), a member of the World Bank Group, to support essential imports and trade-related expenditure.

The Finance Division announced the selection on Tuesday following a competitive bidding process involving nine international financial institutions.

The selected banks are:

- Commerzbank
- J.P. Morgan
- Standard Chartered Bank

The facility will have an initial duration of one year, with the option of two successive annual renewals.

The arrangement represents Pakistan's first transaction under MIGA's Trade Finance programme and marks the first participation of Commerzbank and J.P. Morgan in direct commercial lending to the Pakistani government.

The proposed MIGA guarantee would protect participating lenders against non-payment risks, enabling Pakistan to access international commercial financing with multilateral institutional backing.

Eligible imports include crude oil, petroleum products and liquefied natural gas (LNG), according to the agency's project information reported by Arab News.

Federal Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb held virtual discussions with representatives of the selected banks and MIGA on Tuesday to advance the transaction.

The financing comes amid continued pressure on Pakistan's energy import costs resulting from the Middle East conflict and disruptions to international oil supplies.

The Finance Ministry said the arrangement forms part of the government's strategy to diversify external financing sources, mobilise international private capital and utilise multilateral guarantees while maintaining fiscal discipline.

The government will work with the participating institutions to complete the transaction.
