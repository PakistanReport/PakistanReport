**Karachi, November 5, 2026** — Sindh Chief Minister Syed Murad Ali Shah has travelled to China as part of a provincial initiative to attract foreign investment and expand industrial production.

The delegation includes Senior Minister Sharjeel Inam Memon, Minister Jam Khan Shoro, Agriculture Minister Muhammad Bux Mahar and Special Assistant Syed Qasim Naveed.

### Investment Conference in Shanghai

According to the Sindh government, the province plans to organise an investment conference in Shanghai, with leading Chinese state-owned companies expected to participate.

The visit is focused on attracting investment in **local manufacturing, modern agriculture and pharmaceutical production**.

Shah said stronger economic cooperation with China could help Sindh expand its industrial capacity and introduce modern technologies into key sectors.

### Focus on Industrial Development

The provincial government wants to encourage Chinese businesses to explore manufacturing opportunities in Sindh and strengthen cooperation between investors and local industries.

Agricultural modernisation is another priority, alongside efforts to develop the province's pharmaceutical sector.

The delegation is expected to hold meetings with Chinese representatives to discuss potential investment opportunities.

The visit forms part of Sindh's broader effort to attract foreign capital and strengthen its manufacturing base.
