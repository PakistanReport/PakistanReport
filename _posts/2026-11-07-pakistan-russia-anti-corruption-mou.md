**ISLAMABAD, November 7, 2026 —** Pakistan's National Accountability Bureau (NAB) and the Prosecutor General's Office of the Russian Federation have signed a memorandum of understanding to strengthen cooperation against corruption and money laundering.

The agreement was announced by NAB on November 6 and establishes a framework for information-sharing, professional training, legal cooperation and efforts to trace assets linked to financial crimes.

The memorandum was signed by NAB Chairman Lieutenant General (Retired) Nazir Ahmed and Russian Prosecutor General Aleksandr Vladimirovich Gutsan.

## Information-Sharing and Asset Recovery

According to NAB, the agreement provides for exchanges of expertise and best practices in investigating corruption and money laundering.

The two institutions will also share information about their respective legal systems and legislation, and hold consultations on matters involving mutual legal assistance in criminal cases and law-enforcement cooperation.

Another area covered by the memorandum is the tracing and recovery of assets derived from corruption and money laundering.

Such cooperation can involve complex cross-border legal processes, particularly when assets or financial transactions fall under the jurisdiction of more than one country.

## Training and Institutional Cooperation

The memorandum also provides for international conferences, workshops, working meetings and reciprocal visits by specialists from the two institutions.

Professional training and capacity-building activities are intended to improve the exchange of investigative and legal expertise.

NAB said the two sides would also cooperate on anti-corruption awareness initiatives for young people and undertake joint research on combating corruption.

The agreement represents a formal framework for future institutional engagement. Its practical impact will depend on subsequent cooperation, information exchanges and implementation by the participating authorities.
