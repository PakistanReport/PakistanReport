**HYDERABAD, 18 November 2026 —** Sugar mills in Sindh have yet to formally begin the 2026–27 crushing season despite the provincial cabinet setting November 15 as the start date, raising concerns among farmers over crop losses and delayed wheat cultivation.

The Sindh cabinet approved the date on November 3, but the agriculture department had not issued the required notification as of November 17. The government has also not announced an official sugarcane procurement price.

### Farmers demand timely crushing

The Sindh Abadgar Board (SAB) has urged the government to enforce the crushing schedule and protect growers from financial losses.

SAB President Mahmood Nawaz Shah warned that mature sugarcane loses weight when harvesting is delayed, reducing farmers' earnings because mills purchase the crop by weight.

Growers have demanded a minimum price of Rs647 per 40 kilogrammes, citing rising cultivation costs. The last officially notified rate was Rs425 per 40 kilogrammes for the 2023–24 season.

Under Sindh's sugar factories legislation, mills are required to begin crushing by November 30.

### Mills seek permission for sugar exports

The Pakistan Sugar Mills Association (PSMA) has linked delays to unsold sugar inventories and restrictions on exports.

The association has sought permission to export additional sugar, arguing that surplus stocks and limited storage capacity are affecting mills' ability to purchase newly harvested sugarcane from farmers.

Farmers' representatives maintain that export approvals should not be used to postpone crushing.

### Delay threatens wheat sowing

Sugarcane harvesting normally frees agricultural land for wheat cultivation, making timely crushing important for the next crop cycle.

Sindh produced approximately 2.02 million tonnes of sugar during the 2025–26 season from 19.18 million tonnes of cane crushed, according to provincial figures.

Growers fear the current dispute could push crushing into December, delaying wheat sowing and increasing financial pressure on farming households.
