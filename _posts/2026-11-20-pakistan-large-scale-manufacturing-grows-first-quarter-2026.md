**ISLAMABAD, 20 November 2026 —** Pakistan's large-scale manufacturing (LSM) output increased by **2.07% during July–September 2026**, the first quarter of the current fiscal year, according to provisional figures released by the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics (PBS).

Industrial production also rose **2.45% year-on-year in September** and 3.18% compared with August.

### Automobiles and Garments Lead Growth

Automobile production increased by **32.67%** during the first quarter, while wearing apparel output rose 17.03% and petroleum products grew 12.73%.

Other transport equipment recorded growth of 40.58%, while furniture manufacturing increased by 27.73%.

The Quantum Index of Manufacturing stood at 116.38 for the July–September period, compared with approximately 114.02 in the corresponding quarter of the previous fiscal year.

### Major Industries Record Declines

Despite the overall increase, several important manufacturing sectors reported lower production.

Food manufacturing contracted by **9.43%**, textiles declined 4.08%, pharmaceuticals fell 12.25%, and iron and steel products dropped 11.77%.

Chemical production also decreased by 9.55% during the quarter.

Of the 22 manufacturing sectors monitored, 12 recorded growth while 10 contracted, showing that the improvement was concentrated in selected industries rather than spread evenly across the economy.

The figures highlight the continued importance of automobiles, garments and petroleum products in supporting industrial output as other major sectors face production pressures.
