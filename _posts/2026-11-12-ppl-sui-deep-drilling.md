**KARACHI:** Pakistan Petroleum Limited (PPL) plans to invest between $35 million and $40 million in deep exploratory drilling at the Sui gas field in Balochistan during the 2027–28 financial year.

The proposed well will reach a depth of approximately 6,000 metres and target potential gas resources estimated at two trillion cubic feet, according to an Arif Habib Limited report following the company's analyst briefing on Wednesday.

The exploration project will involve high-temperature and high-pressure conditions, presenting technical challenges for drilling operations.

PPL is pursuing new exploration opportunities as production from its mature fields declines by an estimated 8% to 9% annually.

The company expects overall production to increase from 649 million cubic feet of gas equivalent per day in FY2025–26 to approximately 680 million cubic feet per day in FY2026–27.

Additional output is expected from discoveries at Baragzai in Nashpa, Latif-25, Makori East, Tolanj West and Rehman-9 in the Kirthar Block.

PPL is also developing processing facilities to bring more gas into the domestic supply network.

The Zafir gas processing plant is expected to begin operations in December 2026, initially supplying around 20 million cubic feet of gas per day, with output projected to double during its second phase.

Another facility, the Pateji gas processing plant in Sujawal, is scheduled for commissioning by June 2027 and is expected to add approximately 16 million cubic feet of gas per day.

The planned Sui drilling programme forms part of PPL's efforts to replenish its reserves and offset declining production from existing fields.
