**ISLAMABAD:** Pakistan is expected to unveil its National Data Governance Policy within the next few weeks, Federal Minister for Information Technology Shaza Fatima Khawaja said on Wednesday.

Addressing the Margalla Dialogue in Islamabad, the minister said the policy would establish rules for secure data sharing between government institutions and enable access to anonymised public data for research and innovation.

The Ministry of Information Technology released a draft policy for public consultation in June. It proposes common standards for managing government-held data and reducing the need for citizens to submit the same information to multiple departments.

The proposed framework includes a national data exchange system called WASL, through which institutions would share information while retaining control of their records.

The draft also proposes allowing citizens to access information about data held on them, request corrections and see who has accessed their records. Other provisions cover sensitive data protection, cross-border transfers and oversight of automated decisions.

The Pakistan Digital Authority concluded consultations with federal and provincial stakeholders in August and said implementation would proceed in phases.

Khawaja said separate personal data protection legislation was also being developed.

The policy remains in draft form, with its final provisions and implementation schedule yet to be announced.
