German Ambassador to Pakistan Ina Lepel has called for stronger economic and trade relations between the two countries, highlighting the importance of expanding bilateral cooperation.

She made the remarks during an interactive session at the National Press Club in Islamabad, organised under its *Meet the Press* initiative.

## Focus on economic ties

According to Radio Pakistan, the ambassador emphasised the need to strengthen trade and economic engagement between Pakistan and Germany.

She also acknowledged Pakistan's diplomatic initiatives and its efforts to promote regional peace and dialogue.

The discussion comes amid wider efforts to strengthen commercial and institutional links between the two countries.

## Opportunities for cooperation

Germany remains an important economic partner for Pakistan, particularly through trade, investment and development cooperation.

Greater engagement between businesses, policymakers and educational institutions could help support economic exchanges and long-term partnerships.

The ambassador's remarks underline the importance of sustained dialogue in advancing bilateral relations.
