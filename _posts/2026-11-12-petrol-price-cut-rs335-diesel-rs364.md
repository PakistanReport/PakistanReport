**ISLAMABAD:** The federal government has reduced the price of petrol by Rs3 per litre and high-speed diesel by Rs7 per litre, with the revised rates taking effect on Thursday, 12 November.

According to the Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority (OGRA), petrol now costs Rs335 per litre, down from Rs338, while high-speed diesel has fallen to Rs364 from Rs371 per litre.

The reductions were announced on Wednesday under the government's daily fuel-price adjustment mechanism, which responds to changes in international petroleum markets and related import costs.

Business Recorder reported that the ex-refinery price of petrol declined to Rs240.65 per litre from Rs241.56, while the corresponding diesel price fell to Rs255.88 from Rs263.28.

The petroleum levy remains at Rs80 per litre for both fuels, alongside a climate support levy of Rs5 per litre.

The government introduced more frequent fuel-price revisions amid volatility in global energy markets and concerns over petroleum supplies linked to the Middle East conflict.

Petrol is widely used in motorcycles, rickshaws and private vehicles, while diesel is a major fuel for freight transport, agriculture and other commercial activities.

The lower rates offer some relief to consumers and transport operators, although future prices remain subject to changes in international markets and the government's pricing mechanism.
