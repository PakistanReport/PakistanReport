**ISLAMABAD, November 7, 2026 —** The National Electric Power Regulatory Authority (NEPRA) has provisionally increased the special electricity rate for additional consumption by eligible industrial and agricultural consumers from Rs22.98 to Rs25.30 per unit.

The Rs2.32-per-unit increase applies to electricity consumed above a historical reference level under the government's incremental consumption package.

The revised rate is intended to take effect from the November 2026 billing period, subject to the relevant government notification process.

## Special Rate Aimed at Increasing Electricity Consumption

The incremental consumption package was introduced in December 2025 to encourage industrial and agricultural consumers to use more electricity from the national grid.

Under the scheme, qualifying consumers receive a special rate on electricity consumption exceeding their usage during the reference period from December 2023 to November 2024.

The programme was designed to support industrial activity and improve the utilisation of available electricity generation capacity.

## NEPRA Members Divided Over Decision

The tariff revision was approved following a 2–2 split among NEPRA members, with Chairman Waseem Mukhtar exercising his casting vote in favour of the increase.

Two dissenting members questioned whether the revised rate was supported by sufficient calculations of the actual cost of supplying additional electricity.

The dissenting members called for the package to be suspended until the relevant authorities provided a comprehensive cost assessment.

## Power Division Asked to Provide Further Details

NEPRA has directed the Power Division and relevant power-sector organisations to submit detailed calculations examining the scheme's financial impact and the cost of supplying additional electricity.

The regulator allowed the revised rate on a provisional basis while the assessment remains incomplete. It also indicated that failure to provide the required information could affect the continuation of the programme.

Separately, NEPRA approved a fuel cost adjustment of approximately Rs2.37 per unit for November electricity bills.

The future of the incentive package will depend on the findings of the cost review and any subsequent regulatory decisions.
