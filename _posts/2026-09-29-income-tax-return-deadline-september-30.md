**ISLAMABAD —** Pakistan’s September 30 income-tax return deadline is approaching for individuals and associations of persons, with the Federal Board of Revenue’s published due-date schedule continuing to list the end of September as the filing date for those taxpayer categories.

Under FBR’s standing schedule, individuals and associations of persons, or AOPs, are required to file on or before September 30. Companies generally have until December 31, while companies operating under a special tax year are also listed with a September 30 due date.

The published timetable is the relevant baseline for taxpayers unless FBR issues a separate extension or other formal direction. Requests or calls for additional time do not themselves change the statutory or notified filing position.

### What the deadline covers

An income-tax return records a taxpayer’s income and other required information for the relevant tax year. Depending on the taxpayer’s circumstances, filing can also involve a wealth statement and reconciliation of changes in wealth.

FBR provides electronic filing through its IRIS system. Taxpayers generally need to ensure that information on income, tax deductions, assets and other required fields is complete before submission, particularly where records from employers, banks, businesses or withholding agents have to be reconciled.

The final days before a filing deadline typically bring heavier demand on tax practitioners and online filing systems. Taxpayers who leave reconciliation until the last day can face practical difficulties if records are incomplete or corrections are required.

### Different dates apply to different taxpayers

The September 30 date should not be read as a universal deadline for every company or taxpayer. FBR’s published schedule distinguishes between individuals and AOPs, ordinary companies and companies with special tax years.

For individuals and AOPs, however, September 30 remains the date displayed by FBR. That makes the distinction between a formal extension and public requests for one particularly important: a deadline changes only when the competent authority takes and communicates that decision.

Tax filing deadlines are significant for both taxpayers and the government. For filers, timely submission affects compliance status and can influence the treatment of transactions under Pakistan’s tax regime. For FBR, annual returns provide data used for assessment, risk analysis and enforcement.

### Check the current FBR position before filing

Taxpayers should rely on FBR’s official portal and notifications for any last-minute change in the filing timetable rather than assuming an extension based on past practice or industry requests.

As of the current published schedule, individuals and AOPs face a September 30 due date. With that deadline now imminent, the practical priority is to complete outstanding documentation, reconcile required information and submit through the prescribed filing system within the applicable timeframe.
