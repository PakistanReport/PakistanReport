**ISLAMABAD:** Pakistan will host the Pakistan Minerals Investment Forum 2026 (PMIF26) on November 12 and 13, bringing together international mining companies, investors and government officials to discuss investment opportunities in the country's mineral sector.

The two-day event is being organised in collaboration with the Special Investment Facilitation Council (SIFC) and will focus on mineral exploration, mining projects, processing facilities and investment partnerships.

According to the forum's official programme, discussions will cover financing for new exploration projects, development of mineral resources and infrastructure needed to support mining operations.

## International Companies to Participate

Representatives of major international mining and investment organisations are listed among the forum's speakers and participants.

These include executives from Barrick Mining Corporation, Ivanhoe Mines and International Resources Holding, alongside representatives of the Asian Development Bank and other financial institutions.

Federal Minister for Petroleum Ali Pervaiz Malik is scheduled to address the opening session on November 12. Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar is also listed in the programme.

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif is scheduled to address the forum on November 13, when further announcements and partnership signings are planned.

## Focus on Exploration and Investment

The first day will include discussions on financing early-stage mineral exploration and developing new mining projects.

Sessions will also examine cooperation between Pakistan's Geological Survey and international organisations, including the use of technical expertise and geological data to support exploration.

The second day will focus on investment policies, mining infrastructure and transport links needed to move minerals from remote production sites to domestic and international markets.

The programme includes discussions on electricity, water, rail connections and other facilities required by mining companies.

## Reko Diq and New Mining Projects

Pakistan is seeking to attract greater international investment in its mineral resources, including copper, gold and other commercially valuable deposits.

The Reko Diq copper and gold project in Balochistan is among the major developments drawing international attention to the country's mining sector.

The forum will also feature a technical conference and an exhibition bringing together mining companies, equipment suppliers and industry specialists.

Organisers have scheduled a session for strategic announcements and the exchange of memorandums of understanding on November 13.
