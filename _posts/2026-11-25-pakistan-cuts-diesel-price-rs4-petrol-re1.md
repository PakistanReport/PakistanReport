**ISLAMABAD:** The federal government has reduced the price of high-speed diesel by Rs4 per litre and petrol by Re1 per litre, effective November 25, following changes in international petroleum product prices.

The Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority (OGRA) announced the revised prices on Tuesday under the government's daily petroleum pricing mechanism.

The revised ex-depot prices are:

| Product | Previous price | New price |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Petrol | Rs319 | Rs318 |
| High-speed diesel | Rs357 | Rs353 |

International benchmark petrol prices declined from $83.03 to $81.14 per barrel, while diesel benchmarks fell from $89.12 to $87.78 per barrel, according to pricing details reported by Business Recorder.

The ex-refinery price of petrol decreased from Rs224.61 to Rs222.60 per litre, while that of diesel fell from Rs243.22 to Rs240.10 per litre.

The federal government has retained the petroleum levy at Rs80 per litre on both fuels, alongside a climate support levy of Rs5 per litre.

Under a temporary 30-day pricing arrangement introduced in November, consumers are paying Rs62.55 per litre of the Rs80 petroleum levy on petrol, while oil refineries are absorbing the remaining Rs17.45. The arrangement is expected to impose a cumulative financial burden of Rs15 billion on refineries.

The latest reduction follows a Rs3 per litre cut in petrol prices announced on Monday, when diesel prices remained unchanged.

Petroleum prices remain substantially below their April 3 peaks, when petrol reached Rs458.41 per litre and diesel climbed to Rs520.35 per litre following disruptions to global energy supplies caused by the Middle East conflict.

The government adopted daily petroleum price reviews amid international oil market volatility, replacing the weekly pricing mechanism introduced earlier during the conflict.
