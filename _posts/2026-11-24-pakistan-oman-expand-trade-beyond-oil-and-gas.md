**ISLAMABAD:** Pakistan and Oman have agreed to explore opportunities to diversify bilateral trade and investment beyond the energy sector, following the signing of a petroleum products supply agreement between the two countries.

Federal Minister for Petroleum Ali Pervaiz Malik met Oman's Minister of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion, Anwar bin Hilal Al Jabri, in Muscat on Monday to discuss expanding economic relations.

The ministers agreed to make greater use of the recently established Pakistan–Oman Joint Working Group on Trade and Investment to identify commercial and investment opportunities.

Both sides also agreed to raise visa-related issues with the relevant government ministries to facilitate greater engagement between businesses.

The discussions build on the eighth session of the Pakistan–Oman Joint Ministerial Commission, during which the two countries established a framework for expanding cooperation across several economic sectors.

The ministers highlighted the geographical proximity of Pakistan and Oman and emphasised the importance of developing closer commercial relationships between traders, manufacturers and investors.

The meeting followed the signing of a sales and purchase agreement for petroleum products between the two countries, amid disruptions to regional energy supplies and shipping through the Strait of Hormuz.

The Omani commerce minister expressed hope that the petroleum agreement would establish the foundation for a long-term partnership in the oil and gas sector.

Alongside energy cooperation, both governments are seeking to broaden bilateral commercial relations by encouraging direct engagement between their private sectors and addressing barriers to business activity.
