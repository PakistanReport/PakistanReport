**ISLAMABAD:** The Asian Development Bank (ADB) approved $500 million in contingent disaster financing for Pakistan on Wednesday, providing access to budget support following qualifying natural disasters.

The financing, approved under ADB's policy-based lending programme, is intended to help the government respond to emergencies such as floods and earthquakes without delays in arranging additional funds.

The approval forms part of the second phase of the bank's Climate and Disaster Resilience Enhancement Programme, which supports reforms in disaster preparedness, risk management and financial planning.

According to ADB, Pakistan suffers an estimated $2 billion in economic losses annually from natural disasters.

The bank said the 2022 floods caused $14.9 billion in direct damage and a further $15.2 billion in economic losses.

The programme also aims to incorporate disaster resilience into public investment decisions and strengthen arrangements for financing recovery efforts.

ADB Country Director for Pakistan Emma Fan said greater investment in preparedness could reduce economic losses and protect vulnerable communities.

The latest approval follows a separate $500 million ADB policy-based loan announced in October 2024 to support climate resilience and disaster risk reduction in Pakistan.
