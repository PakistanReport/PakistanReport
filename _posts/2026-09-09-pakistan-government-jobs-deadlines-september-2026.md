Pakistan's National Jobs Portal is carrying a range of federal government vacancies with application deadlines approaching during September 2026.

The opportunities range from management trainee positions for younger applicants to specialist and senior management roles requiring substantial professional experience.

Applicants should check the official National Jobs Portal before applying because vacancies, requirements and deadlines can change.

## 15 Management Trainee positions

National Insurance Company Limited is advertising 15 Management Trainee Officer positions.

The application deadline listed on the National Jobs Portal is September 11, 2026.

The opportunity is particularly notable because it offers multiple vacancies rather than a single senior-level position.

NICL is also advertising several management posts, including roles in human resources, internal audit, takaful, corporate affairs and administration.

Those management vacancies are also listed with a September 11 deadline and generally require relevant professional experience.

## Finance Division vacancies

The Finance Division is recruiting for senior positions.

A Director General position in the Tax Policy Office is listed with an application deadline of September 11.

The post requires significant professional experience.

The Finance Division is also advertising the position of Chief Executive Officer of the Federal Government Pensions Fund Management Company.

The National Jobs Portal lists September 13 as the deadline for that position.

## Ministry of Railways

The Ministry of Railways is recruiting an Advisor at MP-I level.

The position is listed as a contract role and requires at least seven years of relevant experience.

Applications are listed as open until September 12.

## Planning Ministry opportunities

The Ministry of Planning, Development and Special Initiatives has several specialist and project positions available.

A Sector Specialist Agriculture position at PPS-10 level is open until September 14.

Other Planning Ministry opportunities include senior specialist and project leadership roles with deadlines extending to September 15.

These positions generally require substantial professional experience, so applicants should carefully review the eligibility requirements before applying.

## National Tariff Commission vacancies

The National Tariff Commission is advertising specialist positions including Cost Accountant — Forensic & Industrial and Economist.

Both positions are listed with application deadlines of September 16.

The Cost Accountant position requires relevant professional experience, while the Economist role is aimed at a considerably more experienced applicant.

## Education sector opportunities

The Ministry of Federal Education and Professional Training is also advertising senior vacancies.

These include Managing Director of the Daanish Schools Authority, with a September 19 deadline, and the Allama Iqbal Visiting Fellowship Chair at the Centre of South Asian Studies, University of Cambridge, listed until September 25.

Applicants should note that these are specialised senior positions with their own qualification and experience requirements.

## How to apply

Applications for these vacancies are handled through the Government of Pakistan's National Jobs Portal.

Candidates should use the official portal rather than relying solely on social-media posts, forwarded messages or unofficial job websites.

Before submitting an application, check:

- the required education and qualifications;
- minimum professional experience;
- age limits where applicable;
- location and employment type;
- required supporting documents; and
- the exact application deadline.

Candidates should also avoid paying individuals who claim they can guarantee selection for a government job.

## Why applicants should check deadlines carefully

Government vacancies often have relatively short application windows.

Several positions currently listed on the portal close between September 11 and September 16, meaning interested candidates may have only a few days to prepare and submit their applications.

Some older vacancies can also remain visible on listings after their deadlines have passed.

For that reason, applicants should open the individual vacancy page and confirm that applications are still being accepted before submitting their information.
