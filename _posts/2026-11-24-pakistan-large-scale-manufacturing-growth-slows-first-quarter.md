**ISLAMABAD:** Pakistan's large-scale manufacturing (LSM) sector expanded by 2.07 per cent during the first quarter of fiscal year 2026–27, with growth concentrated in garments, automobiles and petroleum products as several major industries recorded declines.

Provisional figures released by the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics (PBS) show that manufacturing output increased by 2.45 per cent year-on-year in September and by 3.18 per cent compared with August.

Garment manufacturing emerged as the largest contributor to quarterly industrial growth, expanding by 17.03 per cent and contributing 2.91 percentage points to the overall manufacturing index.

Automobile production increased by 32.67 per cent, while coke and petroleum products recorded growth of 12.73 per cent.

Other industries reporting substantial gains included transport equipment, furniture and tobacco, with output rising by 40.58 per cent, 27.73 per cent and 22.38 per cent, respectively.

However, ten major manufacturing categories recorded contractions during the July–September period.

Pharmaceutical production declined by 12.25 per cent, iron and steel products by 11.77 per cent, food manufacturing by 9.43 per cent and chemicals by 9.55 per cent.

Textile production, excluding the separately classified wearing apparel sector, contracted by 4.08 per cent, while fertiliser output fell by 7.81 per cent.

Manufacturing growth slowed to 2.07 per cent from 3.9 per cent in the corresponding quarter of the previous fiscal year, although it improved from the 0.4 per cent year-on-year growth recorded during April–June 2026.

Ready-made garment export volumes increased by 17.6 per cent during the first four months of FY27, reflecting continued strength in garment exports despite weakness across other manufacturing industries.
