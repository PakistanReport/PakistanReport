**Islamabad, November 5, 2026** — Pakistan is expected to host the **G-24 Technical Group Meeting in February 2027**, following its assumption of the intergovernmental group's chairmanship on November 1.

Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb reviewed preparations on November 4 during a virtual briefing with G-24 Secretariat Director Dr Iyabo Masha. Saif Ullah Dogar, Senior Adviser to the Executive Director at the International Monetary Fund, also participated.

### Three Priorities for Pakistan

The government has identified three main objectives for its chairmanship:

- **Development-led growth:** Linking economic stability with investment, productivity and employment.
- **Greater representation:** Seeking a stronger role for developing economies in decisions made by international financial institutions.
- **Financial reform:** Advocating affordable development loans, sustainable debt solutions, stronger financial safety nets and climate financing.

### February Meeting Preparations

The February technical meeting is expected to provide policy analysis for subsequent G-24 ministerial discussions held alongside the IMF and World Bank's Spring and Annual Meetings.

Aurangzeb called for early preparations, broad participation and practical policy proposals.

Pakistan succeeded Nigeria as G-24 chair and will coordinate with member countries across Africa, Asia, Latin America and the Caribbean during its tenure.
