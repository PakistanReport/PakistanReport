**ISLAMABAD:** The Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP) has sought export records from seven banks as part of its investigation into the withdrawn initial public offering (IPO) of SE Fruits and Vegetable Limited.

The regulator has also approached 30 banks for details of foreign remittances and other foreign-currency receipts in accounts maintained by the company's sponsors and directors. Some of the banks have submitted their responses, which are being examined.

The investigation was initiated on October 21 under Section 29 of the SECP Act, 1997, read with Section 139 of the Securities Act.

SE Fruits, previously known as Shaheen Enterprises, had planned to offer 30 million shares at a floor price of Rs40 per share. The offering was suspended in October after concerns emerged over financial disclosures and audit information. The company later withdrew its application.

The SECP has also received responses from the issue's joint lead managers, Topline Securities and Growth Securities. Information provided by consultant Parker Russell–A.J.S. is under review.

The Central Depository Company has furnished investor details, while the regulator has sought further records concerning shareholding and transfers involving individuals named in the IPO prospectus. A partial response received on November 2 is being examined.

The company's auditor, Ilyas Saeed & Co., has been given additional time to submit the documents requested by investigators.
