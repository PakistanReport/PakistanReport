**KARACHI:** Topline Securities has forecast that the Pakistan Stock Exchange's benchmark KSE-100 index could reach 220,000 points by December 2027, implying a total return of approximately 25 per cent, including a 7 per cent dividend yield.

In its Pakistan Market Outlook for 2027, the brokerage based its target on a forward price-to-earnings multiple of seven times, reflecting expectations of improving economic conditions.

Topline identified international oil prices and developments in the Middle East, particularly shipping through the Strait of Hormuz, as major factors likely to influence inflation, interest rates and investor confidence.

The brokerage expects Pakistan's economy to grow by 3.5 to 4 per cent during fiscal year 2026–27, followed by 4 to 4.5 per cent in the subsequent fiscal year.

Inflation is projected to average 9.5 to 10.5 per cent in FY27 before easing to between 7 and 8 per cent in FY28.

Topline expects the State Bank's policy rate to remain at 12 per cent during the first half of 2027 before declining to 11 per cent in the second half.

Potential market drivers include progress in International Monetary Fund reviews, stronger foreign exchange reserves, external financing, privatisation and measures to address circular debt.

However, the brokerage warned that domestic political uncertainty, security concerns and geopolitical developments could affect the outlook.

The KSE-100 closed the latest trading week at 177,383 points, down 0.28 per cent, as political uncertainty and profit-taking weighed on shares.
