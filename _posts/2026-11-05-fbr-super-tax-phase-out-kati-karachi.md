**Karachi, November 5, 2026** — Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) Chairman Rashid Mahmood Langrial has said the government plans to gradually abolish the super tax imposed on businesses.

Speaking to members of the **Korangi Association of Trade and Industry (KATI)** on November 4, Langrial said the super tax rate had already been reduced by **two percentage points**, with the process of its eventual elimination now under way.

### New FBR Office for Korangi

Langrial announced plans to establish an **FBR office at KATI** to help industrialists resolve taxation and administrative problems more efficiently.

He also said the FBR would expand its taxpayer help-desk services from Karachi to Sukkur.

The chairman assured businesses that the tax authority would consult stakeholders to address problems involving the import of industrial raw materials.

### Industry Concerns and Tax Compliance

The meeting also covered border-trade issues affecting the leather industry and possible improvements to the pharmaceutical sector's Track and Trace System.

KATI President Sheikh Umer Rehan proposed a joint committee to improve coordination between industrialists and tax officials.

Langrial urged businesses to cooperate with the FBR in identifying tax evasion and improving transparency.

The proposed super tax phase-out would represent a significant change in business taxation, although the government has not announced a timetable for its complete abolition.
