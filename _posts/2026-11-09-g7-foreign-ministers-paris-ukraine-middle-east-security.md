# G7 Foreign Ministers to Meet in Paris for Talks on Ukraine, Middle East and Global Security

**PARIS, November 8, 2026 —** Foreign ministers from the Group of Seven (G7) nations are set to meet in Paris on November 9 and 10 to discuss the wars in Ukraine and the Middle East, energy security, humanitarian assistance and emerging risks associated with artificial intelligence.

The two-day meeting will be held under France's 2026 G7 presidency, with US Secretary of State Marco Rubio among the participants.

France has also invited representatives from Ukraine, Saudi Arabia, Egypt, Brazil and South Korea to take part in discussions.

## Ukraine and Middle East Conflicts on Agenda

According to the French Foreign Ministry, ministers will examine continued military, financial and energy support for Ukraine, alongside diplomatic efforts to address instability in the Middle East.

A separate session on November 10 will focus on assistance to Ukraine, with Ukrainian Foreign Minister Andrii Sybiha expected to attend.

Discussions will include air defence requirements, financial assistance and support for Ukraine's energy infrastructure.

France will also host a fundraising conference on November 9 for repairs to the protective structure at the Chornobyl nuclear power plant, which was damaged during a Russian attack in 2025.

The meeting's broader agenda includes developments in Sudan, the Horn of Africa, Venezuela, Cuba and Haiti, as well as security in the Indo-Pacific region.

## Energy and Food Security

G7 ministers will discuss measures to strengthen international energy supplies and reduce vulnerabilities associated with regional conflicts.

Humanitarian assistance and food security will also feature in the talks.

The French Foreign Ministry said discussions would examine ways to improve the efficiency of humanitarian supply chains, including the distribution and storage of emergency supplies.

World Food Programme Executive Director Luke Lindberg is expected to participate in the humanitarian discussions.

## Artificial Intelligence and Maritime Security

The meeting will address artificial intelligence safety, including international coordination on risks arising from increasingly advanced AI systems.

France said the discussions would build on earlier G7 and United Nations initiatives aimed at supporting safe AI development and deployment.

Maritime and port security will also form part of the wider programme.

A separate gathering of the G7+ Ports Network is scheduled in Marseille to discuss cooperation against drug trafficking and improvements to port security.

## Meeting Relocated to Paris

The foreign ministers' meeting was originally scheduled to take place in Marseille but was moved to Paris shortly before the event.

French authorities cited logistical and operational pressures associated with organising major international gatherings within a limited period.

The change comes amid continuing demonstrations in France over the cost of living and other domestic concerns.

The Paris gathering will be the second formal G7 foreign ministers' meeting held during France's presidency this year.
