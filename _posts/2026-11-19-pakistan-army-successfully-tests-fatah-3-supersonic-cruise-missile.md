**ISLAMABAD, 19 November 2026 —** Pakistan Army has successfully test-launched the **Fatah-3 supersonic cruise missile**, according to the military's media wing, Inter-Services Public Relations (ISPR).

The launch took place on Wednesday and was witnessed by the commander of the Army Rocket Force Command, senior military officers, scientists and engineers.

### Advanced Navigation and Propulsion

ISPR said the missile incorporates advanced navigation and propulsion systems and is designed to strike targets at long range while travelling at supersonic speed.

The military said the new system would improve the Rocket Force Command's operational flexibility and strike capability, strengthening Pakistan's conventional deterrence.

President Asif Ali Zardari, Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif, Field Marshal Syed Asim Munir and the services chiefs congratulated the personnel involved.

The test follows other recent missile developments, including the Fatah-2 extended-range missile tested in October and the locally developed Fa'az air-to-air missile tested earlier this month.
