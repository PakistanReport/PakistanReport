**ISLAMABAD, 16 November 2026 —** Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb has called for greater bank financing for small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs), agriculture, housing and electric vehicles as the government seeks to encourage private-sector investment and economic growth.

The minister made the remarks during a meeting with a Habib Bank Limited (HBL) delegation led by its President and Chief Executive Officer, Muhammad Nassir Salim, according to a Finance Division statement issued on Monday.

The discussions focused on expanding lending to priority sectors, improving financial inclusion and mobilising investment for productive economic activities.

### Financing for businesses and electric vehicles

Aurangzeb emphasised the importance of improving access to credit for smaller businesses, farmers and the housing sector.

He also highlighted financing opportunities under the Pakistan Accelerated Vehicle Electrification (PAVE) Scheme, which forms part of the government's efforts to encourage electric vehicle adoption.

The meeting examined ways to attract private investment into infrastructure projects and strengthen Pakistan's capital markets.

The finance minister stressed the need for suitable financing arrangements for commercially viable projects requiring long-term funding.

### Roshan Digital Account 2.0 discussed

HBL representatives briefed the minister on the bank's international outreach and opportunities to strengthen engagement with overseas markets.

The delegation also discussed Roshan Digital Account (RDA) 2.0, highlighting an encouraging response to expanded facilities and opportunities to increase participation.

Both sides explored closer cooperation to attract investment, facilitate international business activity and improve Pakistan's investment environment.

Aurangzeb urged continued collaboration between the government and banking sector to expand financing opportunities and support private-sector-led economic growth.

The official statement did not announce a new lending allocation, binding investment commitment or implementation timetable arising from the meeting.
