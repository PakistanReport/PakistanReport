**ISLAMABAD —** Pakistan has become the first country to join Hong Kong Customs’ ValidAP trade-document verification platform after Pakistan Single Window operationalised an integration designed to allow electronic trade documents to be checked for authenticity across borders.

The integration became operational on September 25, following an agreement signed earlier this year between Pakistan Customs and the Customs and Excise Department of Hong Kong.

ValidAP is a blockchain-based verification platform developed and hosted by Hong Kong Customs. It uses cryptographic techniques to confirm whether an electronic document has previously been registered by a participating organisation without requiring the underlying document to be stored on the verification platform.

### From agreement to operational integration

Pakistan Customs formally joined the ValidAP project through a memorandum of understanding signed with Hong Kong Customs in May during the World Customs Organization Asia-Pacific Regional Heads of Customs Administrations Conference.

The latest development is the operational connection with Pakistan Single Window rather than the signing of that earlier agreement.

Through the integration, electronic trade documents issued through PSW can be independently checked for authenticity and integrity. The verification process compares a cryptographic hash associated with a document against information registered on the ValidAP system.

Because the verification relies on the document’s digital fingerprint, the document itself and its sensitive contents do not need to be uploaded to the platform for the check.

### Digital verification for cross-border trade

ValidAP was created to address a longstanding problem in international trade: authorities, businesses and other parties often need to confirm that licences, certificates and other documents issued in another jurisdiction are genuine.

Paper-based checks and bilateral verification procedures can add time and administrative work to cross-border transactions. A common digital verification mechanism can allow participating organisations to establish whether a document was issued by the claimed source and whether it has been altered.

Hong Kong’s official ValidAP guidance says the system can support different document formats and a range of trade-related records, including licences, permits and certificates.

Pakistan Single Window already serves as the country’s digital platform for connecting traders with government agencies involved in cross-border trade. Integrating document verification into that architecture extends the system from domestic electronic processing towards cross-border authentication.

### Pakistan is the first overseas participant

Pakistan’s status as the first country to join gives the integration an early demonstration role for the Hong Kong-led project. Hong Kong Customs has promoted ValidAP as an Asia-Pacific digital cooperation initiative under its work within the World Customs Organization regional structure.

The practical value of the system will depend on how widely participating authorities and trading partners use the verification mechanism and how many document types are registered through connected systems.

For Pakistan, the immediate development is nevertheless concrete: the earlier customs cooperation agreement has now moved into an operational PSW integration, allowing electronic trade documents issued through the system to be checked through ValidAP’s cryptographic verification process.
