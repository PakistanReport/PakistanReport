**KARACHI, November 13, 2026 —** Mari Energies Limited has reported a copper-gold discovery at its East Siah Diq exploration site in Balochistan's Chagai district, identifying mineralisation across approximately 1.4 square kilometres.

The company disclosed the findings to the Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX) on Friday.

Its wholly owned subsidiary, Mari Minerals, has completed approximately 25,000 metres of drilling in the Koh-i-Sultan area, around 50 kilometres east of the Reko Diq copper-gold project.

## Drilling Results

The strongest result from the first phase of drilling showed a copper concentration of 0.50% over a 400.25-metre interval, alongside 0.11 grams of gold per tonne of rock.

These figures indicate the concentration of metals in the tested samples, rather than the amount that can be commercially recovered.

The exploration also identified molybdenum, a metal used in steel manufacturing.

## Further Exploration

Drilling for the second phase has been completed, with laboratory results expected in the first quarter of 2027.

Mari Minerals plans additional drilling to determine the deposit's size and depth, including exploration extending to 1,000 metres below the surface.

The company will use the findings to assess the quantity of minerals present and their potential for commercial extraction.
