**KARACHI, November 14, 2026 —** The federal government plans to raise Rs7.25 trillion through domestic debt auctions between November 2026 and January 2027, according to borrowing schedules reported from the State Bank of Pakistan (SBP).

The programme includes Rs6 trillion in Market Treasury Bills (MTBs) and Rs1.25 trillion in fixed-rate Pakistan Investment Bonds (PIBs).

The Treasury bill calendar includes six auctions during the three-month period, with individual targets ranging from Rs400 billion to Rs1.5 trillion.

The government plans to raise Rs1 trillion on November 25, followed by Rs750 billion on December 9 and Rs850 billion on December 23.

Two further Treasury bill auctions, each targeting Rs1.5 trillion, are scheduled for January 6 and January 20.

Approximately Rs6.59 trillion in Treasury bills are expected to mature during the period, highlighting the government's substantial refinancing requirements.

Separately, the government plans to raise Rs1.25 trillion through three fixed-rate PIB auctions, including Rs350 billion in November and Rs450 billion each in December and January.

Treasury bills provide short-term financing, while PIBs allow the government to borrow over longer periods.

The planned Rs7.25 trillion represents gross auction targets rather than an equivalent increase in government debt, as some proceeds will be used to repay existing obligations.

Actual borrowing will depend on investor demand and the bids accepted at each auction.

The results will also provide an indication of the government's financing costs as it manages debt repayments and ongoing budget requirements.
