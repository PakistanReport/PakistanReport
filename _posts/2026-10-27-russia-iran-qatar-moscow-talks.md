**MOSCOW, October 27, 2026:** Russian President Vladimir Putin is expected to hold separate meetings with Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian and Qatar's Emir Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani on Tuesday as Moscow hosts international discussions involving major natural gas producers.

The Kremlin announced the meetings ahead of a gathering of the Gas Exporting Countries Forum, bringing energy cooperation and regional diplomacy into focus during a period of disruption to international fuel markets.

Russia, Iran and Qatar hold substantial natural gas resources, making their policies important to countries dependent on imported energy.

The planned discussions come as instability in the Middle East continues to affect energy transportation routes and raise concerns about future supplies.

For Iran, the Moscow visit provides an opportunity to discuss bilateral economic cooperation and implementation of its existing strategic partnership with Russia.

The precise agenda for Putin's meeting with the Qatari emir has not been publicly detailed.

Qatar is a major exporter of liquefied natural gas, while Russia remains an important international energy producer despite restrictions affecting parts of its trade.

The meetings also take place against a complicated diplomatic background, with governments pursuing different approaches to the conflict involving Iran.

For energy-importing economies, including Pakistan, developments affecting major gas producers can influence fuel availability, import costs and longer-term supply planning.

The significance of Tuesday's meetings will depend on official statements, any agreements announced and the practical commitments emerging from the discussions.
