**ISLAMABAD, November 13, 2026 —** The federal government is seeking to accelerate development of the Nokundi–Gwadar road corridor to improve the transport of minerals from Balochistan's mining areas to Gwadar Port.

Planning Minister Ahsan Iqbal discussed the project with senior representatives of Barrick Mining Corporation on Thursday, according to a statement issued by the Press Information Department.

The proposed corridor would connect mineral-rich areas of Balochistan with port facilities, making it easier to transport mining products for export.

## Government Proposes Joint Working Group

Iqbal proposed forming a working group comprising the Ministry of Planning, National Highway Authority, Balochistan government and mining companies.

The group would work on the project's technical requirements, feasibility, financing and implementation arrangements.

The minister also called for detailed land surveys and three-dimensional mapping to assess a missing section of the proposed route and help determine how it could be developed.

Barrick Mining representatives discussed transport requirements and coordination with government agencies.

## Improving Access to Export Markets

Transport infrastructure is an important consideration for mining companies because large quantities of minerals must be moved from remote extraction sites to processing facilities and ports.

Better road connections could reduce transport difficulties and improve access to international markets for projects in Balochistan.

Iqbal said the mining sector was expected to contribute to Pakistan's goal of increasing total exports to $100 billion under the National Economic Transformation Plan 2035.

The next steps for the Nokundi–Gwadar corridor involve completing technical assessments, identifying financing and agreeing on implementation arrangements.
