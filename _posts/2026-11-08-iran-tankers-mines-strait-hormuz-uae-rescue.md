**DUBAI, November 8, 2026 —** Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) Navy has reported that two oil tankers struck naval mines and caught fire in and near the Strait of Hormuz, amid heightened tensions affecting commercial shipping in the Gulf.

The Iranian military said the incidents occurred after the vessels entered areas it considered unsafe for navigation. The tankers have not been publicly identified, and the reported mine strikes have not been independently confirmed.

## Iran Reports Tanker Incidents

According to the IRGC Navy, one tanker was damaged inside the Strait of Hormuz and another to the east of the waterway.

Iranian authorities claimed the vessels had switched off their navigation and positioning systems and travelled along unauthorised routes before striking mines.

The Strait of Hormuz connects the Persian Gulf with the Gulf of Oman and is a major route for international oil and gas shipments.

The reported incidents come as maritime traffic faces growing security risks amid the confrontation between Iran and the United States.

## UAE Rescues 22 From Burning Cargo Ship

In a separate incident, the United Arab Emirates National Guard announced that its coast guard had rescued 22 people after a fire broke out aboard a cargo ship in the Gulf of Oman.

The National Guard said it received a distress report from the vessel's crew and dispatched specialist teams to the scene.

All 22 people were evacuated, given first aid and handed over to the relevant authorities. The UAE said they were in stable condition.

The cause of the cargo ship fire has not been established, and authorities have not linked it to the tanker incidents reported by Iran.

## Maritime Security Concerns

The latest reports come amid restrictions and military activity affecting shipping around Iran.

US Central Command said American forces had disabled a commercial vessel in the Gulf of Oman after it allegedly attempted to breach Washington's naval blockade.

The combination of reported mine incidents, military operations and shipping restrictions has raised concerns about the safety of vessels using one of the world's principal energy transport routes.
