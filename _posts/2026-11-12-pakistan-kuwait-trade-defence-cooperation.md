**ANTALYA:** Pakistan and Kuwait have agreed to pursue stronger economic and strategic cooperation, with their prime ministers discussing opportunities in trade, investment and energy during a meeting in Türkiye.

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif met Kuwaiti Prime Minister Sheikh Ahmad Al-Abdullah Al-Ahmad Al-Sabah on Wednesday on the sidelines of the COP31 climate summit in Antalya.

According to Pakistan's Prime Minister's Office, the two leaders reviewed bilateral relations and identified business cooperation as an important area for further engagement.

Their discussions covered investment, energy, development cooperation and closer contacts between the people of both countries.

The leaders also welcomed the recent ratification of a bilateral defence cooperation agreement, expressing hope that it would strengthen their broader relationship.

Pakistan's federal cabinet ratified the agreement earlier this month, following Kuwait's approval in July.

The pact provides a framework for closer defence cooperation between the two countries, including areas such as training and technical exchanges.

During Wednesday's meeting, the prime ministers also exchanged views on regional security challenges, climate change and the need for international cooperation on sustainable development.

Both sides expressed support for maintaining regional stability and strengthening economic relations alongside their existing diplomatic and defence ties.

The discussions come as Pakistan seeks to attract foreign investment and expand commercial partnerships with Gulf countries.

The leaders agreed to continue cooperation aimed at expanding bilateral ties and supporting regional development.
