KARACHI: Border Health Services has resumed vaccination arrangements for Umrah pilgrims and other international travellers at a centre near Karachi's Jinnah International Airport after a temporary exemption expired on October 10.

The International Traveller Vaccination Centre at the Civil Dispensary near Star Gate is reported to be operating around the clock, offering applicable travel vaccines and certification services. Requirements vary according to the destination, transit route, age and traveller's circumstances.

Officials cited by The Express Tribune said the facility can provide polio, meningitis and yellow-fever vaccinations where required. Travellers should check the latest rules issued by their destination country and airline rather than assuming that every vaccine applies to every journey.

The report lists a meningitis vaccination fee of Rs2,785 and says polio vaccination is available free of charge. Fees, eligibility and certificate requirements should be reconfirmed with the authorised centre before travel.

Border Health Services has also advised travellers to arrange any necessary documentation before departure to avoid delays. Additional vaccination centres are planned for Hyderabad and Larkana, but their activation should not be assumed until confirmed.
