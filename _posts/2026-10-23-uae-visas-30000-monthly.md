**ISLAMABAD:** The United Arab Emirates is reportedly issuing approximately 30,000 visas to Pakistani nationals every month, according to diplomatic sources cited in a report published on October 22.

The figure suggests that visa processing for Pakistani applicants has improved following a period of difficulties and tighter scrutiny.

The News reported that the estimate relates to visas being issued through online channels, with travel agents also facilitating applications.

However, the figure has not been independently confirmed through publicly released UAE immigration statistics.

Pakistan and the UAE maintain close economic and diplomatic relations, with a substantial Pakistani community living and working in the Gulf country.

The UAE remains an important destination for Pakistanis seeking employment, conducting business or visiting family members.

Visa processing conditions are particularly significant for Pakistani workers because access to Gulf labour markets affects employment opportunities and overseas remittances.

The report also cited diplomatic sources describing an improvement in bilateral relations and a reduction in recent deportation-related concerns.

Applicants should not interpret the reported monthly figure as a guarantee of visa approval.

Individual applications remain subject to UAE immigration requirements, documentation checks and the rules applicable to each visa category.

Travellers and jobseekers should verify the latest requirements through authorised UAE channels before making payments or finalising travel arrangements.
