**ISLAMABAD, 15 November 2026 —** Foreign ministers and senior officials from across the region gathered in Islamabad on Sunday for the 30th Economic Cooperation Organisation (ECO) Council of Ministers meeting, as Pakistan sought to strengthen trade links, transport connectivity and economic cooperation among member states.

Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar is chairing the two-day gathering, which brings together representatives of the ECO's 10 member countries and partner organisations.

Among the visiting officials are Turkmenistan's Foreign Minister Rashid Meredov and deputy foreign ministers from Türkiye, Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan and Kyrgyzstan. Senior representatives of the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation and Organisation of Islamic Cooperation have also arrived.

Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi is expected in Islamabad on Monday, according to Iranian officials.

Pakistan is pursuing the meeting under the theme **“Reviving Regionalism for a Resilient ECO”**, with an emphasis on improving cross-border transport, logistics networks and access to regional markets.

The Foreign Office says Pakistan wants to develop its position as a connectivity hub linking South Asia, Central Asia and neighbouring economies.

Senior officials are reviewing the organisation's programmes and preparing documents for ministerial consideration. Discussions are also expected to address longer-term cooperation on trade, investment and sustainable development.

On the sidelines, Dar met ECO Secretary General Asad Majeed Khan and reaffirmed Pakistan's commitment to hosting an ECO summit in 2027. He also held talks with OIC Secretary General Ismail Ould Cheikh Ahmed on wider regional cooperation.

The ECO comprises Pakistan, Afghanistan, Azerbaijan, Iran, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Türkiye, Turkmenistan and Uzbekistan.

While proposals for stronger transport corridors and trade arrangements feature prominently in the discussions, their practical impact will depend on agreements reached and subsequently implemented by member governments.

The meeting continues on Monday, when further ministerial deliberations and possible outcome documents are expected.
