ISLAMABAD: Pakistan has climbed 17 places to rank 119th in the United Nations E-Government Development Index (EGDI) 2026, reflecting improvements in the country's digital public services and telecommunications infrastructure.

The country's overall index score increased by approximately 23% compared with 2024. Pakistan previously ranked 136th in 2024 and 150th in 2022, representing an improvement of 31 positions over four years.

According to the UN survey, Pakistan recorded an online services score of approximately 84%, reflecting progress in the availability of government services through digital platforms.

Its telecommunications infrastructure score increased by 39%, while its online participation score reached approximately 97%.

Federal Minister for Information Technology and Telecommunication Shaza Fatima Khawaja attributed the improvement to coordinated efforts by federal and provincial institutions to expand digital services and connectivity.

The government has pursued these initiatives under its Digital Nation programme, which seeks to improve public access to government services and strengthen digital infrastructure.

The Digital Nation Pakistan Act, 2025, established the Pakistan Digital Authority to coordinate digital transformation across government institutions.

The UN E-Government Development Index assesses countries using three principal measures: online public services, telecommunications infrastructure and human capital.

The index provides a comparative assessment of digital government development rather than a direct measure of citizens' satisfaction with public services.

Pakistan's higher ranking reflects improvements in the digital government indicators assessed by the UN, particularly online services and telecommunications infrastructure.
