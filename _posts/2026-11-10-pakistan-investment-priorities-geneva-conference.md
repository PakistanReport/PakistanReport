**ISLAMABAD:** Pakistan is participating in the 30th World Investment Conference in Geneva, with Federal Minister for the Board of Investment Qaiser Ahmed Sheikh scheduled to join discussions on attracting foreign capital and maintaining an open investment environment.

The three-day conference, organised by the World Association of Investment Promotion Agencies (WAIPA), opened on Tuesday and runs until 12 November.

According to the organiser's programme, Sheikh is listed among the speakers at a leaders' session examining investment priorities and at a separate discussion on balancing economic security with openness to foreign investors.

### Focus on Investment Opportunities

Pakistan's delegation intends to promote opportunities in information technology, renewable energy, agriculture, mineral exploration and industrial manufacturing.

The government has also outlined plans for meetings with foreign officials, investment representatives and business executives during the conference.

These engagements are intended to introduce potential investors to Pakistan's economic policies and available opportunities.

### Investment Policy Under Discussion

The conference programme covers subjects including industrial policy, sustainable finance, digital infrastructure and supply-chain resilience.

One session involving Sheikh will examine how governments can maintain predictable rules for foreign investors while protecting sensitive economic sectors.

For Pakistan, the discussion is relevant to its efforts to attract long-term foreign direct investment across several industries.
