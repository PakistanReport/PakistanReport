**ISLAMABAD:** Pakistan is hosting the 15th annual meeting of the International Network for Nuclear Security Training and Support Centres (NSSC Network) from November 23 to 26, bringing together representatives of the International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) and 49 member states.

The four-day gathering, organised in collaboration with the IAEA, will focus on strengthening nuclear security training, improving technical cooperation and sharing expertise among participating countries.

According to Pakistan's Foreign Office, the meeting will provide an opportunity to exchange experiences and explore ways to build sustainable national capabilities in nuclear security.

The NSSC Network was established in 2012 to support cooperation among national centres responsible for nuclear security training and technical assistance.

These centres help authorities develop specialist personnel, maintain security equipment and access scientific and technical expertise needed to protect nuclear and other radioactive materials.

The network also promotes information sharing and the exchange of best practices among participating institutions.

Pakistan previously hosted the network's annual meeting in 2016, becoming the first country to host the gathering outside the IAEA's headquarters in Vienna.

The Foreign Office said the latest meeting reflects Pakistan's continuing contribution to international nuclear security through cooperation, knowledge sharing and capacity building.

The meeting comes amid continuing international efforts to strengthen nuclear security systems and maintain institutional expertise through training and technical collaboration.
