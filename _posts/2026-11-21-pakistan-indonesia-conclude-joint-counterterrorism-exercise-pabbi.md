**RAWALPINDI:** The Pakistan and Indonesian armies have concluded a joint counterterrorism exercise at the National Counter Terrorism Centre (NCTC) in Pabbi, aimed at strengthening operational cooperation and exchanging military expertise.

The exercise, named Elang Strike-III, was the third edition of the bilateral training programme between the two countries.

According to the Inter-Services Public Relations (ISPR), the exercise began on November 9 and concluded on Saturday, November 21.

Personnel from both armies participated in training activities designed to improve counterterrorism tactics, operational procedures and combat capabilities.

The programme focused on sharing practical experience, refining operational drills and exchanging best practices developed through counterterrorism operations.

The military said the joint training provided an opportunity for participating personnel to improve coordination and learn from each other's operational experience.

ISPR described the exercise as a reflection of longstanding defence cooperation between Pakistan and Indonesia and said both countries remained committed to strengthening their bilateral military relationship.

The latest programme follows a series of joint military exercises conducted by Pakistan with other partner countries, including Tajikistan, Russia, Oman and Sri Lanka.

Elang Strike-III concluded as both countries continue to develop military-to-military cooperation through joint training and professional exchanges.
