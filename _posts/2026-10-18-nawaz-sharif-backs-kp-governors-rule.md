Pakistan Muslim League-Nawaz (PML-N) President Nawaz Sharif has defended the imposition of emergency and governor's rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KP), saying the intervention should have happened earlier, while Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has reiterated that the federal government considered the move necessary because of security concerns.

The two leaders made their remarks on Saturday, October 17, nearly a week after the federal authorities imposed emergency and governor's rule in the province on October 11. Their comments were reported separately; they were **not** delivered at a joint press conference.

## Nawaz Sharif says action should have come sooner

Speaking to journalists in London, Nawaz Sharif criticised the former Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf (PTI) provincial administration and argued that the security situation warranted earlier intervention. [Geo News reported](https://www.geo.tv/latest/687030-nawaz-hits-out-at-pti-govt-says-emergency-should-have-been-imposed-in-kp-earlier) his remarks on October 17.

His criticism of PTI and assessment of the province's governance are political positions attributed to him, not independent findings by Pakistan Report.

In separate remarks in Gujranwala, Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif said the Centre had been compelled to act because, in its view, the provincial government had failed to cooperate adequately in counterterrorism efforts. [Dawn reported](https://www.dawn.com/news/2037836/centre-was-compelled-to-impose-emergency-in-kp-pm-shehbaz-reiterates) the prime minister's defence of the measure and his account of earlier dialogue efforts.

## The decision and the opposition response

The federal authorities announced the emergency and governor's rule on October 11. In an [official Radio Pakistan account](https://radio.gov.pk/11-10-2026/president-promulgates-emergency-governors-rule-in-kp), Law Minister Azam Nazeer Tarar said the measures had been promulgated under Articles 232 and 234 of the Constitution and that provincial executive powers had shifted to the governor. The federal government cited counterterrorism and governance concerns.

PTI has rejected the intervention as politically motivated and challenged its legitimacy. [Reuters reported](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/pakistan-president-imposes-emergency-province-ruled-by-ex-pm-imran-khans-party-2026-10-11/) the party's objections and the federal government's stated security rationale when the measure was introduced.

Parliament endorsed the measures during the week amid opposition protests, according to [Dawn's October 18 coverage](https://www.dawn.com/news/2037989). The latest statements by the two Sharifs are a political follow-up to the October 11 decision, not an announcement of a second or newly imposed emergency.

The dispute centres on whether the federal government's security justification warranted replacing an elected provincial administration, a question on which the government and PTI continue to offer sharply different accounts.
