**ISLAMABAD:** Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar chaired a meeting on Wednesday to review Pakistan's compliance with international labour conventions and the implementation of related domestic laws.

The committee examined commitments under International Labour Organization (ILO) conventions and the regulatory measures required to meet international standards.

Dar directed federal departments to coordinate with provincial governments and meet agreed timelines for fulfilling these obligations.

Compliance with international labour conventions is also relevant to Pakistan's preferential trade access to the European Union under the GSP+ scheme, which links tariff concessions to the implementation of international agreements on labour rights and other standards.

The EU's revised GSP framework, scheduled to take effect in January 2027, will require continued compliance with applicable international conventions.

The meeting was attended by the prime minister's adviser on industries and production, Special Assistant Tariq Bajwa, and senior officials from the commerce, overseas Pakistanis and human rights ministries.
