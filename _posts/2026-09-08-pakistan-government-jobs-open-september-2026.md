Several federal government and public-sector job opportunities remain open in Pakistan this September, with vacancies ranging from management trainee positions to senior specialist and executive roles.

Pakistan's National Jobs Portal currently lists opportunities across the federal government and public-sector organizations, with a number of application deadlines approaching over the next several days.

## National Insurance Company vacancies

National Insurance Company Limited is recruiting for several positions.

One of the largest current openings is for **Management Trainee Officers**, with **15 vacancies** listed and an application deadline of **September 11, 2026**.

Other NICL positions listed with the same deadline include:

- Manager Human Resource
- Manager Internal Audit
- Manager Takaful
- Manager Corporate Affairs
- Manager Administration

Candidates should check the individual vacancy requirements before applying, as eligibility, experience and qualification requirements differ by position.

## Finance Division opportunities

The Finance Division is also advertising senior positions.

An opening for **CEO of the Federal Government Pensions Fund Management Company** has an application deadline of **September 13, 2026** and requires substantial professional experience.

A **Director General position in the Tax Policy Office** is listed with a September 11 deadline and requires at least 12 years of experience.

These are senior-level roles rather than entry-level vacancies.

## Planning Ministry vacancies

The Ministry of Planning, Development and Special Initiatives has several specialist positions listed.

These include a **Sector Specialist Agriculture** position with a September 14 deadline and a **Senior Specialist (Energy)** position with a September 15 deadline.

A Project Director/Executive Director vacancy is also listed with applications open until September 15.

## Other federal opportunities

Other currently listed positions include an Advisor position with the Ministry of Railways and vacancies associated with the National Tariff Commission and Ministry of Interior and Narcotics Control.

Applicants should pay close attention to the closing date for each vacancy because deadlines differ.

## How to apply

Applicants should use Pakistan's official National Jobs Portal to check the complete job description, required qualifications, experience criteria and application deadline.

Applications should be submitted through the official process specified for each vacancy.

Job seekers should avoid relying on screenshots or social-media posts alone when applying for government positions. Vacancy requirements and deadlines should always be checked against the official listing.

## Why it matters

Government and public-sector vacancies attract substantial interest in Pakistan, but information about available positions is often scattered across advertisements and social-media posts.

Using official job portals makes it easier for applicants to verify whether a vacancy is genuine and whether applications are still open.

Pakistan Report will periodically highlight verified employment opportunities where the information can be traced to official recruitment platforms.

**Important:** Vacancies can be amended, withdrawn or closed. Applicants should verify the latest status, eligibility requirements and deadline on the official portal before submitting an application.
