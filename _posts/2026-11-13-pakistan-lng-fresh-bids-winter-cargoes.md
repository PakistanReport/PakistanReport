**ISLAMABAD, November 13, 2026 —** Pakistan LNG Limited (PLL) has invited fresh international bids for two liquefied natural gas (LNG) cargoes after rejecting earlier offers priced as high as $31 per million British thermal units (MMBtu).

The state-owned company is seeking the shipments to meet domestic gas requirements during the winter months, when demand typically increases.

According to the reported tender details, the first cargo is scheduled for delivery between December 14 and 16, 2026, while the second is expected between January 9 and 11, 2027.

Both shipments are to be delivered to Port Qasim, Karachi, on a Delivered Ex-Ship basis. International suppliers have until November 18 to submit their bids.

## Earlier LNG Offers Rejected

PLL previously received offers from TotalEnergies Gas & Power Limited and QatarEnergy Trading LLC for the same delivery periods.

For the December shipment, TotalEnergies quoted approximately $27.30 per MMBtu, while QatarEnergy offered $31 per MMBtu.

TotalEnergies also submitted the lowest evaluated offer for the January cargo at approximately $27.10 per MMBtu.

However, PLL declined the offers and initiated a fresh bidding process in an effort to secure more competitive prices.

## Winter Gas Supply Planning

Pakistan relies on imported LNG alongside domestic gas production to meet demand from households, industries and power producers.

The government is also seeking supplies under existing long-term arrangements with Qatar as regional disruptions create uncertainty in international energy markets.

The renewed tender forms part of efforts to arrange adequate gas supplies while managing the higher costs associated with spot-market purchases.

The next stage will be the submission and evaluation of fresh bids before any procurement decision is made.
