**KARACHI, November 7, 2026 —** Bank Alfalah Limited has completed the sale and transfer of its Afghanistan banking operations to Ghazanfar Bank, ending its presence in the country after more than two decades.

The transaction was completed on November 5 following regulatory approvals and the fulfilment of conditions under the sale agreement.

## Kabul and Herat Operations Transferred

The transfer covers Bank Alfalah's branches in Kabul and Herat, together with their associated assets and liabilities.

The bank said it had received the necessary approvals from the State Bank of Pakistan and Da Afghanistan Bank, Afghanistan's central bank.

Following completion of the transaction, Bank Alfalah ceased banking operations in Afghanistan. The two branches will be closed in accordance with regulatory requirements.

## Arrangements for Customers

Before the transfer, Bank Alfalah notified customers of arrangements for their deposits and accounts.

Customers could withdraw their funds, close their accounts or transfer balances to Ghazanfar Bank. Under the announced arrangements, balances remaining after the specified deadline would be transferred unless customers had provided alternative instructions.

The measures were intended to maintain access to banking services during the transition.

## End of a Two-Decade Presence

Bank Alfalah entered Afghanistan in 2005 and operated there for approximately 21 years.

The bank continues to maintain operations in Pakistan and other international markets, including Bangladesh, Bahrain and the United Arab Emirates.
