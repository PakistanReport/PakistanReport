**ISLAMABAD:** The federal government has announced another reduction in petrol and high-speed diesel prices, providing modest relief to motorists, transport operators and businesses across Pakistan.

According to a notification issued by the Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority (OGRA), the price of petrol has been reduced by Rs3 per litre, from Rs335 to Rs332, effective Friday, November 13.

High-speed diesel has become cheaper by Rs4 per litre, bringing its price down from Rs364 to Rs360.

The latest adjustment follows reductions announced for November 12, when petrol fell by Rs3 and diesel by Rs7 per litre.

The revised rates were calculated under the petroleum pricing mechanism approved by the federal government, which provides for daily price adjustments.

Changes in international fuel benchmarks, freight premiums and other pricing costs contributed to the latest revision.

Petrol is widely used in motorcycles, cars and rickshaws, while diesel is a major fuel for freight transport, passenger vehicles and agricultural machinery.

The lower rates are expected to provide some immediate savings for fuel consumers, although the reductions remain relatively small compared with overall transport and operating expenses.

Government taxes and duties remain a substantial component of retail fuel prices, with the latest adjustment leaving the main petroleum levies and fixed marketing margins unchanged.

The new prices apply for November 13 under the current daily pricing system.
