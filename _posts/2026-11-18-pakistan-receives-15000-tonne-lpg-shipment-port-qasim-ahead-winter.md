**KARACHI, 18 November 2026 —** Pakistan has received a 15,000-metric-tonne shipment of liquefied petroleum gas (LPG) at Port Qasim as authorities seek to strengthen fuel supplies ahead of winter.

Imported by SSGC LPG Limited (SLL), the cargo berthed at Engro Vopak Terminal Limited. The Petroleum Division described it as the largest LPG shipment received in Pakistan.

The delivery follows revised government guidelines aimed at encouraging state-owned companies to increase LPG imports and improve domestic availability.

### Revised rules to boost LPG imports

According to the Petroleum Division, previous pricing restrictions discouraged formal-sector companies from importing LPG because they could not recover procurement costs.

The government has since revised the pricing framework and encouraged state-owned enterprises to take a greater role in imports.

SLL, a subsidiary of Sui Southern Gas Company, is among the companies supporting these efforts.

Officials expect the changes to improve supplies, particularly in remote and mountainous areas where LPG is widely used during winter.

### Supply disruptions increase import needs

The shipment follows disruptions to LPG supplies from Iran, a major source of imported fuel for Pakistan.

Border trade restrictions in October interrupted deliveries and contributed to rising cylinder prices.

Pakistan consumes approximately 2.4 million tonnes of LPG annually and imports more than half its requirements, according to figures cited by Petroleum Minister Ali Pervaiz Malik.

### Further shipments expected

A ceremony at Port Qasim marked the cargo's arrival, attended by senior Petroleum Division officials, SLL Managing Director Jawad Ali Khan and representatives of the Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority and Engro Vopak Terminal.

Special Secretary Petroleum Mirza Nasiruddin Mashhood said the government was working to ensure adequate LPG supplies at affordable prices.

The Petroleum Division expects further LPG shipments in the coming weeks to support domestic demand during winter.
