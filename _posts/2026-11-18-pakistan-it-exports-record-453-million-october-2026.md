**KARACHI, 18 November 2026 —** Pakistan's information technology exports reached a record $453 million in October 2026, rising 17.4% from $386 million a year earlier, according to State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) data cited by industry analysts.

Exports of telecommunications, computer and information services increased 4.1% from September's $435 million, surpassing the previous monthly record of $437 million set in December 2025.

During July–October of the current financial year, IT export earnings totalled $1.699 billion, compared with approximately $1.445 billion in the same period last year.

### Net IT exports reach $373 million

According to Topline Securities, net IT exports reached $373 million in October, up 11% year-on-year, after deducting payments for imported IT services.

On a trailing 12-month basis, IT exports stood at approximately $4.9 billion, highlighting the sector's growing contribution to Pakistan's foreign exchange earnings.

### Banking reforms and export targets

The SBP has identified freelancing as an important driver of IT export growth and introduced measures to simplify international payments for technology companies and independent professionals.

These include streamlined documentation, faster processing of eligible transactions and permission for exporters to retain 50% of their proceeds or $5,000 per month, whichever is higher, in designated foreign currency accounts.

Pakistan's IT exports reached approximately $4.6 billion in the previous financial year, falling short of the government's $5 billion target.

The Ministry of Finance projects IT exports of $5.5 billion for FY2026–27, while the Uraan Pakistan economic plan targets $10 billion by FY2028–29.

Despite the latest record, the SBP has identified difficulties in scaling technology businesses as a continuing challenge. Sustaining growth will require stronger technical skills and greater access to higher-value international markets.
