Pakistan International Airlines has temporarily suspended flights to Riyadh amid safety concerns and operational disruption following missile and drone attacks affecting Saudi Arabia.

A PIA spokesperson advised passengers to confirm their flight status through the airline’s call centre before travelling to the airport. The Pakistan Airports Authority confirmed the Riyadh suspension and said other PIA services to Saudi Arabia were continuing.

Other international airlines have also cancelled services, while European aviation authorities widened warnings about Saudi airspace. No confirmed date for resuming PIA’s Riyadh flights was established in the reporting reviewed. Travellers should verify individual bookings directly.
