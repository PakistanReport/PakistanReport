**KARACHI, 18 November 2026 —** Pakistani conglomerate Dany Group has acquired the former residence of the United States consul general in Karachi's Civil Lines area in a transaction reportedly valued at Rs6.92 billion, or approximately $25 million.

Bloomberg first reported the sale on Tuesday, citing people familiar with the transaction.

The US State Department confirmed to Bloomberg that the property had been sold to Dany Tameerat, the conglomerate's real estate division, but did not disclose the purchase price. Dany Group also confirmed the acquisition without revealing its financial terms.

### Historic estate spans 250,000 square feet

The property covers approximately 250,000 square feet, equivalent to 2.32 hectares, in Civil Lines, one of Karachi's established residential districts.

The neighbourhood contains several prominent historic buildings, including the former residence of Pakistan's founder, Muhammad Ali Jinnah, and the Sind Club.

Dany Group is a privately held Karachi-based conglomerate with interests in consumer electronics and property development. Its businesses include Dany Technologies and Dany Tameerat.

The reported transaction is among the country's larger recent high-value property deals, although the purchase price has not been officially confirmed.

### Future development plans under discussion

Dany Tameerat Chief Executive Officer Danish A. Rauf described the former diplomatic residence as a historically significant structure that adds value to the estate.

He told Bloomberg that the company was discussing possible partnerships with local and international organisations for future projects at the site.

Rauf indicated that the former residence would remain central to any development, although the company has not announced a final project or construction timetable.

The acquisition marks a significant addition to Dany Group's property portfolio, with the site's eventual use yet to be determined.
