**ISLAMABAD:** Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar has directed government ministries to improve coordination on economic diplomacy as Pakistan seeks to expand its exports and strengthen commercial ties with international partners.

The directions came during a meeting in Islamabad on Tuesday to review the government's economic diplomacy priorities and progress on engagement with foreign countries.

According to the government's Press Information Department, Dar emphasised closer cooperation among relevant ministries and departments to advance Pakistan's economic interests.

He also called for regular follow-up on diplomatic initiatives to ensure that engagements with foreign partners produce practical results. Senior officials from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs attended the meeting.

Pakistani missions abroad play a role in facilitating contacts with foreign governments and businesses, while trade-related departments are responsible for supporting exporters and addressing commercial barriers.

Closer coordination between these institutions could help ensure that opportunities identified through diplomatic engagement are communicated to the relevant authorities and businesses.

The latest directions focus on improving coordination and follow-up within the government's existing economic diplomacy efforts.
