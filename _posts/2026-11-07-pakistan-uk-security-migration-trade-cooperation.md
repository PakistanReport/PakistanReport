**ISLAMABAD, November 7, 2026 —** Pakistan and the United Kingdom have agreed to strengthen bilateral cooperation in security, law enforcement, migration, trade and investment following talks between senior officials in Islamabad.

Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar met British Minister of State for Security Dan Jarvis on November 5 to review relations between the two countries and discuss areas for further engagement.

According to Pakistan's Foreign Office, both sides agreed to deepen cooperation across several sectors and exchanged views on regional and international developments.

## Security and Migration Cooperation

Security, law enforcement and migration were among the principal areas discussed during the meeting.

The two sides also reviewed cooperation under the R4+E4 framework, which provides a forum for engagement on regional and international issues.

Dar emphasised the importance of continued cooperation between Pakistan and the United Kingdom on bilateral matters and developments of mutual interest.

The Foreign Office statement did not announce a new treaty or provide an implementation timetable for the areas discussed.

## Further Talks on Cybercrime and Counterterrorism

The discussions were followed by a separate meeting on November 6 between Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi and Dan Jarvis.

According to the official government account, the two sides agreed to expand cooperation in counterterrorism, anti-narcotics, cybercrime, immigration, identity-related services, coast guard matters and training programmes.

They also agreed to convene a meeting of the working group established between their interior ministries.

The two countries decided to finalise a memorandum of understanding on anti-narcotics cooperation, intended to strengthen operational links between Pakistan's Anti-Narcotics Force and the UK's National Crime Agency.


## Trade and Investment Relations

Trade and investment were also identified as areas for closer engagement during the meeting between Dar and Jarvis.

The discussions reflect the broader scope of Pakistan–UK relations, which extend beyond security matters to economic and diplomatic cooperation.

The meetings established areas for continued cooperation, with further progress dependent on subsequent discussions and implementation by the relevant institutions.
