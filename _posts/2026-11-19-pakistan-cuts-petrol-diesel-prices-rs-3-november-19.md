**ISLAMABAD, 19 November 2026 —** Pakistan has reduced petrol and high-speed diesel prices by Rs. 3 per litre each, bringing the new rates to **Rs. 322 and Rs. 355**, respectively, effective Thursday.

The Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority (OGRA) announced the revised prices on Wednesday under the federal government's approved petroleum pricing mechanism.

| Fuel | Previous price | New price | Reduction |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Petrol | Rs. 325 | **Rs. 322** | Rs. 3 |
| High-speed diesel | Rs. 358 | **Rs. 355** | Rs. 3 |

*All prices are per litre.*

### Falling Global Oil Prices

The latest reduction follows a separate Rs. 3 cut in petrol prices on Tuesday, when diesel remained unchanged.

The adjustment comes amid declining international petroleum prices. According to market reporting, global petrol and diesel benchmarks have eased, reducing domestic supply costs.

The government has also moved to a daily fuel-price review system to respond more quickly to international market fluctuations.

Lower petrol prices offer some relief to motorists, while cheaper diesel may help reduce operating costs for transporters, farmers and businesses.

The latest adjustment brings petrol down by **Rs. 6 per litre over two consecutive revisions**, while diesel has fallen by Rs. 3 over the same period.
