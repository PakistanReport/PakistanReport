**ISLAMABAD, 20 November 2026 —** Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif and President Asif Ali Zardari have agreed to resolve outstanding political and national issues through dialogue and consultation amid growing differences between coalition partners.

The two leaders met at Aiwan-e-Sadr on Thursday, with Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi also attending the discussions.

According to a statement from the Presidency, the meeting reviewed the country's political situation, with participants emphasising the need to address disagreements through mutual understanding.

### Debate Over New Provinces Raises Tensions

The meeting followed disagreements between the Pakistan Muslim League-Nawaz (PML-N) and Pakistan Peoples Party (PPP) over proposals to create new provinces and administrative units.

PPP Chairman Bilawal Bhutto Zardari has opposed any attempt to divide Sindh without the consent of its people, while expressing support for a South Punjab province through the relevant constitutional process.

Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi had earlier advocated discussions on creating new administrative units, including the possibility of seeking public opinion through a referendum.

The proposals have prompted debate over provincial boundaries, constitutional requirements and the role of political consensus.

### Coalition Partners Emphasise Consultation

The prime minister and president agreed that outstanding matters should be addressed through negotiations involving relevant stakeholders.

PPP Senator Sherry Rehman separately clarified that Bilawal Bhutto Zardari remained committed to the coalition but had concerns about insufficient consultation on important national decisions.

She also said his earlier remarks about possible unrest in Sindh were intended to highlight concerns over political decisions made without public consultation.

The discussions at Aiwan-e-Sadr reflected efforts to ease tensions between the coalition partners and maintain cooperation on national and political matters.
