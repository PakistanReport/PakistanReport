**KARACHI, 16 November 2026 —** IGI Investments (Private) Limited is considering acquiring up to 60% of Nimir Resins Limited, according to a disclosure submitted by its parent company, IGI Holdings, to the Pakistan Stock Exchange on Monday.

IGI Investments' board has granted preliminary approval to evaluate the acquisition and conduct due diligence.

The proposed purchase involves shares held by Rudolf Pakistan (Private) Limited and five individual shareholders: Zafar Mahmood, Khalid Qazi, Imran Afzal, Umar Iqbal and Aamir Jamil.

The company's chief executive officer and chief financial officer have been authorised to negotiate terms and appoint legal and financial advisers.

Any transaction remains subject to satisfactory due diligence, agreement on price and structure, definitive contracts and required regulatory approvals.

Nimir Resins, established in 1964, manufactures industrial chemicals used in textiles, paints, paper and composite materials.

If completed at the proposed level, the acquisition would give IGI Investments a controlling stake in the listed chemicals manufacturer.
