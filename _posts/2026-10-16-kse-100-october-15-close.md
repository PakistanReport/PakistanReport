Pakistan's benchmark KSE-100 index ended trading on Thursday, 15 October, at 166,186.16 points, a gain of 768.66 points or 0.46%, according to the Pakistan Stock Exchange's official index data.

The index traded between 165,613.11 and 166,877.01 points during the session, indicating that it finished below its intraday high despite closing in positive territory.

## Second consecutive gain

Market reporting described the advance as the second successive positive session, with buying interest lifting the index earlier in the day before some gains were surrendered.

The broader All Share Index also rose, closing at 100,823.27 points, up 477.21 points or 0.48%, according to the exchange.

## What the figures show

A daily rise reflects the movement of the benchmark during one trading session; it does not establish a sustained recovery or predict the direction of future trading.

Investors continue to assess domestic economic conditions and geopolitical developments. Specific explanations for the day's price movements should be attributed to market participants rather than treated as confirmed causes.

The figures in this report relate exclusively to the close on 15 October, not to earlier weekly market reports.
