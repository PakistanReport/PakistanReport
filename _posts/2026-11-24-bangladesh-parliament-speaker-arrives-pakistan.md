**ISLAMABAD:** Bangladesh Parliament Speaker Hafiz Uddin Ahmad arrived in Islamabad on Monday for a six-day official visit aimed at expanding parliamentary cooperation and strengthening bilateral relations between Pakistan and Bangladesh.

The six-member delegation was received at Islamabad International Airport by National Assembly Speaker Sardar Ayaz Sadiq, who extended the invitation for the visit.

According to Pakistan's National Assembly Secretariat, this is the first official visit to Pakistan by a sitting speaker of the Bangladeshi parliament.

The delegation is scheduled to meet Pakistan's president, prime minister, Senate chairman and members of Parliament to discuss bilateral relations, legislative cooperation and regional developments.

A meeting with the Pakistan–Bangladesh Parliamentary Friendship Group is scheduled for Tuesday, following a briefing involving Pakistan's Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Ministry of Commerce.

The delegation includes Bangladeshi lawmaker Jahrat Adib Chowdhury, who was recently elected to the executive committee of the Inter-Parliamentary Union (IPU), a Geneva-based global organisation of national parliaments, with support from Pakistan.

Another visiting Bangladeshi lawmaker, Humam Quader Chowdhury, is the grandson of A.K.M. Fazlul Quader Choudhury, who served as Pakistan's National Assembly speaker from 1963 to 1965.

Bangladesh's current Parliament Speaker, Hafiz Uddin Ahmad, is a graduate of the Pakistan Military Academy and has expressed a desire to revisit the institution during his stay.

The delegation's programme includes visits to Lahore and Karachi, where meetings are planned with provincial assembly speakers and other officials, alongside visits to historical and cultural sites.

Bangladeshi officials have identified closer legislative engagement, cooperation at international parliamentary forums and stronger people-to-people connections as key objectives of the visit.
