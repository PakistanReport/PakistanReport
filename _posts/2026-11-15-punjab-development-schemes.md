**LAHORE, 15 November 2026 —** Punjab's Provincial Development Working Party (PDWP) has approved 19 development schemes worth Rs126.668 billion, covering healthcare, education, urban infrastructure, traffic management and industrial development.

The approvals were granted at the PDWP's 13th meeting, held on Friday under the chairmanship of Punjab Planning and Development Board Chairman Dr Naeem Rauf.

A major component of the package focuses on improving basic municipal infrastructure through the Punjab Development Programme. Projects include sewerage networks, stormwater drainage, street paving and related facilities in several cities.

The approved locations include Mailsi, Abdul Hakim, Ferozewala, Daska, Kamoke, Kot Abdul Malik, Muridke, Ali Pur Chatha, Hazro, Renala Khurd and Mian Channu, alongside other urban areas.

Healthcare projects include a revised Rs10.143 billion scheme for a children's hospital in Rawalpindi and a Rs1.507 billion project to establish a 50-bed hospital in Bhurban, Murree.

In higher education, the committee approved Rs18.862 billion for infrastructure, academic facilities and operational requirements at the Punjab University of Technology, Rasul, in Mandi Bahauddin.

Other approvals include Rs3.246 billion for a Provincial Traffic Management and Command Centre at Qurban Police Lines in Lahore and Rs6.881 billion for the revised second phase of the MPA Hostel in the provincial capital.

The development portfolio also includes Rs3.979 billion for aquaculture malls and Rs2 billion for a programme intended to upgrade technology in the cutlery industry.

Separately, the committee approved Rs4.063 billion for remodelling Jhika Gali Chowk in Murree.

The meeting was attended by Planning and Development Board Secretary Rafaqat Ali, Chief Economist Dr Farrukh Naveed and officials from the departments concerned.
