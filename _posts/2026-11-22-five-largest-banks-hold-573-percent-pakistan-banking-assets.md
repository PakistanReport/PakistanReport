**KARACHI:** Pakistan's five largest banks account for 57.3 per cent of the banking sector's total assets and 53 per cent of deposits, according to the latest quarterly statistics released by the State Bank of Pakistan (SBP).

The figures, covering the period ending September 30, 2026, highlight the concentration of financial resources among a small number of major banks.

The SBP's Quarterly Compendium of Banking Statistics shows that the five largest banks also held 62.1 per cent of total banking investments.

Banks ranked sixth to tenth accounted for another 23.9 per cent of total assets, while those ranked eleventh to twentieth held 13.3 per cent.

Government securities accounted for approximately 96 per cent of the banking industry's investments, reflecting the sector's substantial exposure to government borrowing.

The five largest banks held 47.9 per cent of private-sector advances, while banks ranked sixth to tenth accounted for 27.5 per cent.

The concentration of deposits and investments highlights the significant role of major institutions in Pakistan's financial system, particularly in financing government borrowing and extending credit to businesses.
