**LAHORE, November 8, 2026 —** Nestlé Pakistan is increasing its reliance on locally sourced materials and strengthening inventory management to manage global supply disruptions, rising input costs and foreign-exchange risks.

The company outlined these measures in its financial report for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, during which it recorded growth in sales and profitability.

## Nine-Month Financial Performance

Nestlé Pakistan reported a net profit of Rs14.53 billion for January–September 2026, up 3.1% from Rs14.10 billion in the corresponding period last year.

Net sales increased 6.3% to Rs160.26 billion from Rs150.82 billion, while operating profit rose 5.9% to Rs28.61 billion.

Earnings per share increased to Rs320.31 from Rs310.81. The company's board also approved an interim cash dividend of Rs97 per share.

## Focus on Local Sourcing

Nestlé Pakistan said it had accelerated efforts to source materials domestically while maintaining inventories of critical inputs needed for manufacturing.

The measures are intended to reduce dependence on international supply chains and limit exposure to foreign-exchange fluctuations.

The company did not provide a detailed breakdown of the materials covered by its localisation efforts.

## Managing External Pressures

Nestlé Pakistan identified geopolitical uncertainty, higher fuel and energy prices, and inflation as challenges affecting its operations.

The company maintained a cautious outlook for the remainder of 2026, citing continued volatility in energy and other input costs and its potential effect on consumer spending.
