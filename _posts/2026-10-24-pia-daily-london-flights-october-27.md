**ISLAMABAD:** Pakistan International Airlines (PIA) plans to operate a daily service to London from October 27, 2026, as it expands its flight schedule between Pakistan and the United Kingdom.

The planned timetable comprises five weekly flights from Islamabad and two from Lahore, providing a combined seven flights a week to London Heathrow.

The flights are expected to use Terminal 4 at Heathrow, according to the announced arrangements.

### More Connections for Travellers

The additional frequency is intended to provide passengers with more options for travelling between Pakistan and London, a major destination for Pakistani families, students and business travellers.

The announcement concerns services scheduled to begin on October 27. It does not mean the daily timetable was already operating on October 24.

Travellers should confirm flight dates, terminal information and booking availability directly with PIA before making travel arrangements, as airline schedules can change.
