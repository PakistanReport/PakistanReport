Oman’s Foreign Minister Sayyid Badr Albusaidi is scheduled to visit Pakistan on October 13 and 14 at the invitation of Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar, Pakistan’s Foreign Office announced.

The programme includes the eighth session of the Pakistan–Oman Joint Ministerial Commission in Islamabad, to be co-chaired by the two foreign ministers. The commission provides a forum for reviewing bilateral relations and identifying areas for closer cooperation.

Preparatory discussions have covered trade, investment, agriculture, energy, labour, aviation, education and other sectors. The Omani minister is also expected to call on Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif.

The announced itinerary should not be mistaken for completed meetings or signed agreements. Any outcomes of the visit will require separate confirmation from the governments.
