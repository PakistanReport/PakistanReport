**PESHAWAR, November 8, 2026 —** The Special Investment Facilitation Council (SIFC) and the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa government have discussed measures to simplify business regulations, improve industrial infrastructure and attract investment to the province.

An SIFC delegation met the provincial chief secretary in Peshawar, according to a statement issued on November 7. The meeting focused on business facilitation, regulatory reforms and the development of special economic zones.

## Business Facilitation and Regulatory Reforms

SIFC officials discussed initiatives aimed at simplifying business registration, licensing and other administrative procedures.

These included the Asaan Karobar Act, the National Business Portal and Business Facilitation Centres, which are intended to reduce regulatory hurdles and make government services more accessible to businesses.

The council acknowledged the provincial government's adoption of the Asaan Karobar Act.

## Development of Special Economic Zones

The meeting reviewed investment opportunities and infrastructure requirements at three special economic zones: Hattar, Rashakai and Daraban.

Officials discussed improving the zones' operational efficiency and investment readiness to support industrial activity and encourage private-sector participation.

The discussions also addressed coordination between federal and provincial authorities to resolve administrative difficulties affecting investors.

The Khyber Pakhtunkhwa chief secretary assured the SIFC delegation of the provincial government's cooperation in implementing business reforms and facilitating industrial investment.
