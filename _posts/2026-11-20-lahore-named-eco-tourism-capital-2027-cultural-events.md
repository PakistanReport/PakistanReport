**LAHORE, 20 November 2026 —** Lahore has officially received the title of **Economic Cooperation Organisation (ECO) Tourism Capital for 2027**, with Punjab planning a year-long programme of cultural activities, heritage celebrations and international tourism events.

The designation was formally handed over during a ceremony in Shusha, Azerbaijan, where ECO Secretary General Ambassador Asad Majeed Khan presented the title to Punjab Tourism Secretary Socrat Aman Rana.

Shusha held the designation for 2026.

### Cultural Festivals and Heritage Events Planned

Punjab officials said the proposed 2027 programme would feature international delegations, cultural festivals, traditional performances, handicraft exhibitions and events highlighting Lahore's historic architecture.

The Lahore Biennale, culinary festivals and activities celebrating the city's Mughal, Sikh and other cultural heritage are among the planned attractions.

Tourism promotion efforts will focus on landmarks including Lahore Fort, the Walled City, museums, archaeological sites and religious heritage buildings.

A Pakistani tourism pavilion at the handover ceremony also showcased the country's historical landmarks, natural landscapes and cultural traditions.

### Punjab Seeks Greater Tourism Investment

Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz described the designation as an opportunity to introduce Punjab's cultural heritage to a wider international audience.

Senior Minister Marriyum Aurangzeb said the government intended to use the recognition to encourage heritage conservation, tourism infrastructure development, investment and cultural exchanges.

The ECO initiative promotes tourism cooperation among member countries and aims to strengthen regional cultural connections.

Lahore was also named the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation's Tourism and Cultural Capital for 2026–27, giving the city two regional platforms for promoting its tourism attractions.
