**ISLAMABAD:** Pakistani students who won medals in international scientific competitions were presented with cash awards at a World Science Day ceremony in Islamabad on Tuesday.

Federal Minister for Science and Technology Khalid Hussain Magsi distributed the prizes during an event organised by the Pakistan Science Foundation in collaboration with UNESCO and other scientific institutions.

The gathering, held at the Ministry of Science and Technology, brought together scientists, diplomats, researchers and government officials to discuss international cooperation in science.

A roundtable examined how countries could work together on climate change, water security, public health, emerging technologies and disaster preparedness.

Representatives from UNESCO, Jordan's embassy, the Italian Agency for Development Cooperation and Pakistani government institutions took part in the discussion.

Another session focused on the participation of women in scientific research and leadership.

Speakers called for better opportunities for women researchers, stronger mentoring arrangements and greater representation in scientific institutions and decision-making.

The panel included Rwanda's High Commissioner Fatou Harerimana, Fatima Jinnah Women University Vice Chancellor Prof. Bushra Mirza and representatives of the ECO Science Foundation, the United Nations University and the National Commission on the Status of Women.

Pakistan Science Foundation Chairman Dr Muhammad Akmal and senior officials from the Ministry of Science and Technology also addressed the gathering.

The event marked World Science Day for Peace and Development, observed internationally on November 10. This year's theme was *Common Ground Through Science Diplomacy*.

The programme was organised as part of the International Decade of Sciences for Sustainable Development, which runs from 2024 to 2033.
