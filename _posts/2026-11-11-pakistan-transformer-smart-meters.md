**ISLAMABAD:** The federal government is working to arrange financing for smart meters on electricity transformers across all power distribution companies, aiming to improve the monitoring of electricity losses and address complaints of theft and overbilling.

Federal Minister for Economic Affairs Ahad Cheema reviewed the proposed installation programme at a meeting in Islamabad on Tuesday, alongside ongoing development projects involving power distribution companies, commonly known as DISCOs.

Officials informed the meeting that progress had been made in securing funding through the Economic Affairs Division.

Transformer-level smart meters would allow distribution companies to monitor electricity supplied to different parts of their networks more accurately, helping them identify discrepancies and investigate possible losses.

The government expects the system to improve billing accuracy and strengthen oversight of electricity distribution.

Cheema also directed officials to make arrangements for the project to continue if distribution companies are transferred to private ownership.

Under the proposed approach, financing agreements and development programmes still being negotiated or prepared at the time of privatisation would be transferred to the incoming investor, subject to agreed conditions.

Negotiations with multilateral development partners will continue until the privatisation process is completed.

The minister said new projects undertaken by private investors would have to follow competitive bidding procedures and obtain approval from the National Electric Power Regulatory Authority where required.

He also stressed that privatisation should not impose additional financial pressure on electricity consumers, while investors would remain responsible for meeting their contractual and financial commitments.

Federal Minister for Power Sardar Awais Ahmad Khan Leghari, the prime minister's adviser on privatisation and senior officials from relevant ministries attended the meeting.

The government has not announced the total cost of the smart-meter programme, the number of transformers to be covered or a timetable for installations.
