**TEHRAN, October 26, 2026:** Iran says diplomatic communications with the United States are continuing through intermediaries despite stalled negotiations and rising concerns about renewed military confrontation in the Middle East.

Iranian officials have indicated that Tehran remains open to diplomatic engagement, even as major disagreements with Washington remain unresolved.

The latest statements come amid disputes over the Strait of Hormuz, US restrictions affecting Iranian shipping and broader demands concerning regional security.

Iran recently proposed arrangements that could allow shipping through the strategic waterway to resume under certain conditions, according to reporting by the Associated Press.

Washington has not accepted the proposal on the terms put forward by Tehran, leaving the prospects for a wider agreement uncertain.

The Strait of Hormuz remains central to the dispute because it is a major route for international energy shipments. Restrictions and security threats in the area have contributed to volatility in global oil markets.

Pakistan has played a diplomatic role in earlier efforts to facilitate communication between Tehran and Washington, alongside other regional partners.

The continued exchange of messages suggests that diplomatic channels have not been completely closed, even though formal negotiations have made limited progress.

However, communication through intermediaries does not amount to an agreement or a confirmed resumption of direct talks.

Any breakthrough would depend on whether the two sides can narrow their differences over shipping access, security arrangements and the conditions for broader negotiations.
