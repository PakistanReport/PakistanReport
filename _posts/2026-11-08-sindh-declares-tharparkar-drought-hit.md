**KARACHI, November 8, 2026 —** The Sindh government has decided to declare Tharparkar a calamity-hit district following drought conditions that have damaged crops and threatened livelihoods across the desert region.

The decision was taken at a meeting chaired by Chief Minister Syed Murad Ali Shah in Karachi on November 7.

The chief minister directed provincial authorities to restore drinking-water facilities, address shortages affecting local communities and develop longer-term measures to improve water availability.

## Severe Crop Losses Across Tharparkar

Officials reviewing the situation reported widespread agricultural damage across all seven talukas of Tharparkar.

According to details of the chief minister's meeting reported in the press, an estimated 85% to 90% of cultivated crops had been destroyed or rendered unproductive, with only a small proportion surviving.

Inadequate and uneven rainfall has affected crop development and natural vegetation, creating additional difficulties for households dependent on agriculture and livestock.

The scale of the losses has raised concerns about food security, household incomes and the availability of fodder for animals.

## Immediate Restoration of Drinking-Water Facilities

Chief Minister Murad Ali Shah ordered the urgent repair and reactivation of non-functional reverse osmosis plants used to provide drinking water.

He also instructed the administration to restore existing water-supply sources and address faults preventing communities from receiving water.

Access to reliable drinking water is a central concern in Tharparkar, where many settlements depend on a combination of groundwater treatment facilities and local supply systems.

The provincial government's directives call for these facilities to be made operational, although a completion timetable has not been announced.

## Long-Term Water Supply From Naukot to Mithi

The chief minister also called for long-term planning to improve the water supply from Naukot to Mithi.

Officials have been asked to examine a more sustainable arrangement for supplying water to Mithi, including the technical and administrative requirements of the proposed infrastructure.

## Livestock and Rural Livelihoods at Risk

Drought conditions also threaten livestock, an important source of income and household support in Tharparkar.

The provincial administration has been directed to address fodder shortages and coordinate relief measures for affected communities.

The chief minister called for continued monitoring of conditions and the restoration of essential services.

The effectiveness of the response will depend on how quickly water facilities are repaired, relief supplies reach affected settlements and longer-term water projects move beyond planning.
