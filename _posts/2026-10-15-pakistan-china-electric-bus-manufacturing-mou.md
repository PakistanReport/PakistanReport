Pakistan's GUGO Motors and China's Shengtang Group have signed a memorandum of understanding (MoU) to explore a joint venture for manufacturing electric buses in Pakistan, according to reporting published on 15 October 2026.

The signing ceremony took place in Islamabad on Wednesday, 14 October. Haroon Akhtar Khan, an adviser to the prime minister, attended as chief guest, according to Business Recorder. The event was organised by the Pakistan–China Business Forum.

The proposed collaboration is intended to explore technology transfer, the development of domestic manufacturing capacity and the possibility of producing vehicles for export under a Made in Pakistan approach.

## An agreement to explore, not a completed factory

An MoU signals the companies' intention to cooperate, but it is **not proof that a manufacturing plant has been built, financed or begun production**. Publicly reported details do not establish a confirmed factory site, investment value, annual output target or commercial launch date.

Those distinctions matter because an electric-bus production venture would require facilities, component sourcing, skilled labour, testing and an after-sales service network before locally assembled buses could reach customers.

If the proposal advances, it could contribute to Pakistan's efforts to develop electric-vehicle manufacturing and reduce dependence on imported finished vehicles. The scale of any economic benefit would depend on actual investment, local content and sales, none of which can be established from the signing alone.

## What happens next

The key milestones to watch are a binding joint-venture agreement, financing commitments, a verified plant location and a published production timetable. Until then, the development is best described as a **proposed partnership**.

The agreement and its stated objectives were reported by [Business Recorder on 15 October](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40444298/). A separately published company announcement specifying financial and operational terms was not independently verified for this report.
