Exports are an important source of foreign currency for Pakistan. They support jobs in agriculture, manufacturing, transport and other industries.

When export earnings decline, businesses may have less income to invest in production, while the country receives fewer dollars from overseas sales.

Pakistan's exporters also face challenges linked to electricity costs, international demand and competition from other countries.

The government has been working to support export growth and reduce obstacles faced by businesses seeking international customers.

Improving transport connections, keeping production costs competitive and finding new buyers can help Pakistani companies expand their presence abroad.

The latest figures show why stronger exports remain an important economic priority, particularly as Pakistan works to increase foreign exchange earnings and develop more reliable access to international markets.
