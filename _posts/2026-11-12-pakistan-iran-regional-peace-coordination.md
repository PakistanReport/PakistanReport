**ANTALYA:** Pakistan and Iran have agreed to strengthen diplomatic coordination on regional security as Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif called for restraint and dialogue to help end the prolonged conflict in the Middle East.

Shehbaz met Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian on Wednesday on the sidelines of the COP31 climate summit in Antalya, Türkiye.

According to Pakistan's Prime Minister's Office, the leaders discussed the regional security situation and reaffirmed their intention to expand bilateral cooperation.

Shehbaz expressed concern over the continuing conflict and said sustained diplomatic engagement was necessary to prevent further escalation and achieve lasting stability.

He also emphasised the importance of building on the understandings reached under the Islamabad memorandum of understanding in June 2026.

The prime minister reiterated Pakistan's willingness to support diplomatic efforts aimed at reducing regional tensions.

Pezeshkian welcomed Pakistan's diplomatic support for Iran and its efforts to promote regional peace, according to the official account of the meeting.

The Iranian president also expressed interest in expanding cooperation between the neighbouring countries across several sectors.

Both leaders agreed to maintain regular consultations and closer coordination on shared challenges and emerging opportunities.

Information Minister Attaullah Tarar and Senator Sherry Rehman were among the Pakistani officials attending the meeting.

Pakistan has sought to maintain a diplomatic role in efforts to reduce tensions involving Iran and other regional actors.
