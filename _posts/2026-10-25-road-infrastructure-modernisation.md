**LAHORE:** Federal Minister for Communications Abdul Aleem Khan has outlined the government's plans to modernise Pakistan's road infrastructure, with an emphasis on expanding motorway connectivity, improving transport facilities and supporting economic development.

Speaking to members of the business community in Lahore, the minister highlighted the importance of an efficient national road network for trade, industry and regional connectivity.

He said improvements to highways and motorways would help businesses transport goods more efficiently and strengthen links between major economic centres.

## Expanding motorway connectivity

Abdul Aleem Khan discussed the government's plans to develop and improve major road connections across the country.

He emphasised the need for modern infrastructure that can accommodate increasing traffic volumes and support the movement of commercial vehicles.

Motorway development remains an important component of Pakistan's transport strategy, particularly for connecting industrial centres, agricultural regions and major cities.

The minister also highlighted the role of better road infrastructure in supporting tourism and improving access to different parts of the country.

## Supporting business and economic activity

During his interaction with business representatives, the minister stressed that transport infrastructure plays a significant role in economic growth.

Reliable highways can reduce travel difficulties for businesses, improve access to markets and support the movement of agricultural and industrial products.

He said the government was focused on infrastructure improvements that would strengthen commercial activity and create better opportunities for investment.

## Improving the national road network

The minister reiterated the government's commitment to developing a more efficient and modern road network.

He highlighted the importance of continued infrastructure investment and coordination to improve connectivity across Pakistan.

The plans form part of broader efforts to strengthen the country's transport system and support long-term economic development.
