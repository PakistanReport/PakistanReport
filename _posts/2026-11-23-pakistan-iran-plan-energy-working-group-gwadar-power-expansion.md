**ISLAMABAD:** Pakistan and Iran are preparing to establish a joint working group on electricity and energy, with financing for the second phase of Gwadar's electricity transmission line among the main issues expected to be discussed.

The initiative follows an agreement reached during the 22nd session of the Pakistan-Iran Joint Commission for Economic Cooperation to strengthen coordination on bilateral energy projects.

According to *Business Recorder*, the Iranian Embassy in Islamabad has submitted the proposed agenda and delegation details to Pakistan's Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

Discussions are expected to cover electricity generation, transmission and distribution, including technical cooperation, equipment supplies and maintenance services.

Both countries also plan to explore renewable energy investment, electricity storage systems and opportunities for Iranian companies to participate in Pakistan's power sector.

Iran has nominated representatives from its Ministry of Energy, Renewable Energy and Energy Efficiency Organisation, MAPNA Group and Niroo Research Institute.
