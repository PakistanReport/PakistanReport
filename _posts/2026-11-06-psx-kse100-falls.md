**KARACHI, November 6, 2026 —** The Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX) ended Friday's trading session in negative territory, with the benchmark KSE-100 Index falling 1,493.18 points, or 0.85%, to close at 173,886.68.

The decline came after the index had finished the previous session at 175,379.86 points, according to official PSX market data.

During Friday's session, the benchmark reached an intraday high of 174,369.19 points before falling as low as 172,809.89.

The index remained below its previous closing level throughout the session and finished the day below the 174,000-point mark.

## Trading Activity

The PSX reported approximately 1.48 billion shares traded in the regular market, with a total value of Rs22.57 billion.

The KSE-100 Index itself recorded trading volume of approximately 195.8 million shares.

Other major indices also ended lower. The KSE-30 Index declined by 833.18 points, or 1.61%, to 50,762.89, while the All-Share Index lost 830.04 points, or 0.79%, to close at 104,193.89.

The broader decline indicates that the day's weakness was not confined to the benchmark's largest companies.

Market reporting pointed to profit-taking and weaker international equity sentiment as possible influences on Friday's performance. However, the official closing figures alone do not establish a single cause for the decline.

Despite the day's losses, the KSE-100 remained above its October 30 closing level, reflecting gains accumulated earlier in the week.

Investors will be watching whether the market stabilises when trading resumes following the weekend.
