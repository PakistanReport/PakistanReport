**KARACHI, 16 November 2026 —** Gul Ahmed Textile Mills Limited has approved the issuance of a letter of intent to procure two wind turbines with a combined generation capacity of 15.75 megawatts, extending the company's plans to diversify its energy sources.

In a disclosure to the Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX) on Monday, the textile manufacturer said its board had authorised the proposed procurement through a resolution passed by circulation on 14 November.

The two turbines, each rated at 7.875MW, would be sourced from Ming Yang Renewable Energy (International) Company Limited through Windtek (Private) Limited.

The announcement represents approval to proceed with a letter of intent rather than confirmation that a final purchase agreement has been signed.

### Earlier renewable energy projects

The latest decision follows a series of renewable-energy approvals announced by Gul Ahmed in October.

Those plans included a separate 7.5MW wind turbine from Goldwind through Orient Energy Systems, along with a solar installation of up to 6MW using panels supplied by JinkoSolar.

The company also approved a 20-megawatt-hour battery energy storage system from Sungrow, with Orient Energy Systems acting as the supplier.

Together, these proposals indicate a broader effort to develop renewable generation and electricity storage capacity alongside the company's textile operations.
