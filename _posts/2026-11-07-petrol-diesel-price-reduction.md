**ISLAMABAD, November 7, 2026 —** The federal government has reduced the price of petrol by Rs6.59 per litre to Rs346 and high-speed diesel by Rs4.25 per litre to Rs378, with the revised rates taking effect on Saturday, November 7.

According to an announcement by the Petroleum Division, the new prices will remain applicable for four days, until November 10.

The latest revision lowers the price of petrol from Rs352.59 per litre and high-speed diesel from Rs382.25 per litre.

## Revised Petroleum Prices

| Petroleum product | Previous price | New price | Reduction |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Petrol | Rs352.59 | Rs346.00 | Rs6.59 |
| High-speed diesel | Rs382.25 | Rs378.00 | Rs4.25 |

*All prices are per litre.*

The four-day pricing period differs from the usual fortnightly adjustment schedule. The revised rates cover November 7–10, with November 9 falling on a public holiday.

Petrol is widely used by motorists and motorcyclists, while high-speed diesel is a major fuel for freight transport, buses and agricultural machinery.

Lower fuel prices can reduce operating costs for transport operators and households. However, any wider effect on fares or consumer prices will depend on whether businesses pass the reductions on to customers.

The government has not announced prices for the period beginning November 11 in the cited notification. Further adjustments will depend on the next official review.
