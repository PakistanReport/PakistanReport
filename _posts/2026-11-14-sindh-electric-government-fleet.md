**KARACHI, November 14, 2026 —** The Sindh government has decided to gradually replace petrol- and diesel-powered vehicles used by provincial departments with electric vehicles (EVs), aiming to reduce fuel expenditure and promote cleaner transport.

The decision was taken during a meeting of the provincial Cabinet Committee on Austerity, chaired by Senior Minister Sharjeel Inam Memon and attended by Local Government Minister Syed Nasir Hussain Shah, provincial secretaries and other officials.

The committee reviewed measures to control government spending, conserve fuel and assess the transport requirements of different departments.

It also approved vehicle purchases for the Home Department, Anti-Corruption Establishment and Universities and Boards Department, while agreeing to give preference to electric models in future government procurement.

Memon, who also holds the information, transport and mass transit portfolios, said reducing expenditure and protecting public resources remained important priorities for the provincial administration.

He said a gradual transition towards electric vehicles could lower routine operating costs, reduce dependence on conventional fuels and support environmental protection.

The government intends to extend the transition across its official transport fleet over time rather than replace all existing vehicles immediately.

Shah also supported greater adoption of electric vehicles by local government institutions, highlighting the impact of conventional vehicle emissions on air quality in urban areas.

The policy comes as provincial authorities seek ways to reduce recurring expenses while encouraging the use of cleaner transport technology.

Further details on procurement and implementation will determine the pace at which electric vehicles enter Sindh's government fleet.
