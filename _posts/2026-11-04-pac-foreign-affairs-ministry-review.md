Pakistan's National Assembly scheduled the 55th meeting of its Public Accounts Committee (PAC) for November 4, with matters concerning the Ministry of Foreign Affairs on the agenda.

According to the National Assembly's official meeting notice, the revised meeting was scheduled for 2:30 p.m. in Committee Room No. 2 at Parliament House, Islamabad.

## Parliamentary oversight of public spending

The Public Accounts Committee is responsible for examining government expenditure and reviewing financial accountability across federal institutions.

Its oversight work includes considering audit observations and seeking explanations from government departments about the use of public funds.

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs is responsible for Pakistan's diplomatic operations, including overseas missions and international representation.

## Committee's role

The PAC brings together lawmakers to scrutinise public financial management and recommend corrective action where necessary.

The November 4 meeting forms part of the committee's ongoing review of federal ministries and departments.
