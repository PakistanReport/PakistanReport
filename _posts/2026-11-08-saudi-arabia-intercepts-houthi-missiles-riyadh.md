**RIYADH, November 8, 2026 —** Saudi Arabia's air defences intercepted and destroyed two ballistic missiles launched by Yemen's Houthi movement towards Riyadh on Sunday, according to the Saudi-led coalition.

Loud explosions were heard in the capital after Saudi Civil Defense issued a warning alert for the Riyadh region. Authorities subsequently announced that the immediate danger had passed.

The coalition later reported intercepting two cruise missiles in the Riyadh and southern regions.

## Missile Interceptions Across Saudi Arabia

Coalition spokesperson Major-General Turki al-Maliki confirmed the interception of the two ballistic missiles targeting Riyadh.

Saudi authorities reported another ballistic missile interception in the kingdom's Eastern Province earlier on Sunday.

The incidents follow a series of missile and drone attacks directed at Saudi territory in recent months, including airports, ports and energy infrastructure.

## Fighting Intensifies in Yemen

The latest interceptions come amid renewed hostilities between the Saudi-led coalition and Yemen's Houthi movement.

The fighting has undermined a United Nations-brokered truce established in 2022, with both sides carrying out military operations since hostilities resumed earlier this year.

On Saturday, Yemen's Houthi-run health ministry said Saudi airstrikes had killed at least 33 people near the capital, Sanaa.

Saudi authorities said they were investigating the reported incident. The casualty figures have not been independently verified.

The renewed confrontation is taking place against the backdrop of wider regional tensions involving Iran, the United States and Israel.
