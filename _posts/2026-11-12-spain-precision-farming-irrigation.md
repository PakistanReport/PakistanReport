**ISLAMABAD:** Spain has offered to share technical expertise with Pakistan in precision agriculture, modern irrigation and sustainable water management as the two countries explore closer cooperation in the agricultural sector.

The offer was made during a meeting between Federal Minister for National Food Security and Research Rana Tanveer Hussain and Spanish Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food Luis Planas Puchades in Türkiye on Wednesday.

According to Pakistan's Ministry of National Food Security and Research, the ministers discussed opportunities in olive cultivation, olive oil production, agricultural research and the adoption of modern farming technologies.

The talks covered agricultural mechanisation, precision farming, efficient irrigation systems and technologies for reusing water.

Spain also welcomed Pakistan's proposal to send a technical delegation to study advanced agricultural practices and meet relevant institutions.

Rana Tanveer highlighted Pakistan's potential to expand olive cultivation and develop its domestic olive oil industry.

He said cooperation with Spain could help improve orchard management, processing techniques, product quality and access to international markets.

The minister also emphasised the importance of water-efficient farming technologies as Pakistan faces increasing water scarcity and climate-related pressures.

Both countries discussed strengthening links between agricultural research institutions and technical specialists to support training, technology exchange and agricultural innovation.

The meeting ended with a commitment to continue cooperation.
