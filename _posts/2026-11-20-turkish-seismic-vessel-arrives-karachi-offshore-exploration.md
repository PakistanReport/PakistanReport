**KARACHI, 20 November 2026 —** Türkiye's seismic research vessel **Oruç Reis** has arrived in Karachi to begin offshore oil and gas exploration surveys, marking a new phase in energy cooperation between Pakistan and Türkiye.

Turkish Energy Minister Alparslan Bayraktar joined Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar and Petroleum Minister Ali Pervaiz Malik at a ceremony welcoming the vessel on Friday.

The Turkish-built ship will gather seismic data to help identify potential oil and gas deposits in Pakistan's offshore waters.

### Surveys Planned Off Karachi and Makran Coast

The vessel will initially conduct a **three-dimensional seismic survey covering 670 square kilometres** in the Indus-C Block off Karachi.

It will subsequently carry out a two-dimensional survey covering **2,353 kilometres of survey lines** in offshore blocks along the Makran coast.

According to Türkiye's Energy Ministry, the first survey is expected to take approximately 50 days, followed by around 25 days for the second phase.

The 87-metre vessel, built in 2017, previously conducted offshore exploration surveys near Somalia.

### Pakistan Seeks to Expand Domestic Energy Exploration

The operation follows Pakistan's award of **23 offshore exploration blocks in 2025**, its first such auction in nearly two decades.

Türkiye's state-owned petroleum company secured a 25% stake and operatorship in an exploration block off Karachi.

Pakistan is seeking to develop domestic oil and gas resources to reduce its dependence on imported energy.

Turkish officials have also indicated plans to begin offshore drilling in Pakistan in 2027, following assessment of the seismic survey findings.

The surveys will help determine whether further exploration is warranted, although commercially viable oil and gas reserves have not yet been established.
