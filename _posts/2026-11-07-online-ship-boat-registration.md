**ISLAMABAD, November 6, 2026 —** Pakistan has made its online Ship and Boat Registration System operational, allowing vessel owners to submit registration applications electronically instead of visiting government offices.

Federal Minister for Maritime Affairs Muhammad Junaid Anwar Chaudhry announced the launch on Friday, saying the new system would reduce paperwork, save applicants time and improve transparency in maritime administration.

The online facility covers registration applications for ships and boats overseen by the Mercantile Marine Department (MMD), which operates under the Ministry of Maritime Affairs.

The department is responsible for registering merchant ships, fishing boats, sailing vessels and harbour craft under Pakistan's maritime laws.

## Digital Payments Planned

Although online registration applications are now available, electronic payment of registration fees is still being developed.

According to the ministry, officials from the MMD and the State Bank of Pakistan are expected to discuss integrating a digital payment mechanism.

The government aims to introduce online fee payments within 15 days, enabling applicants to pay through a single electronic payment link.

The minister said the system would also help maintain a more comprehensive national database of registered vessels and their owners.

Such records could support fisheries management and coastal disaster preparedness by helping authorities identify fishing communities vulnerable to cyclones and other extreme weather events.
