**ISLAMABAD:** Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi has directed authorities to begin work on converting One Constitution Avenue into an international-standard hotel from January 1, 2027, as the capital prepares to host the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) summit next year.

The direction was issued at a meeting on Tuesday to review hotel accommodation and related facilities in Islamabad.

Officials briefed the minister on the proposed conversion of the property, which the government plans to undertake in consultation with an international hotel chain. A shopping mall is also planned in the adjoining building.

Naqvi asked the relevant departments to speed up work on hotels already under construction and ensure that existing properties are upgraded before the summit.

He directed officials to remain in contact with hotel owners and help resolve issues holding up their projects. Cooperation between local operators and international hotel chains was also discussed.

The meeting reviewed renovation work at the Islamabad Convention Centre, including a proposal for an additional hall.

Naqvi also called for hospitality training programmes to prepare staff for the new and upgraded facilities, directing officials to coordinate with training institutions.

The government's plans for One Constitution Avenue were announced earlier this month, following a federal cabinet decision concerning the property.

Pakistan is scheduled to host the SCO Council of Heads of State summit in Islamabad in 2027.
