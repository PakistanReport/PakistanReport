**ISLAMABAD, 18 November 2026 —** Pakistan's Ministry of Commerce has agreed to support Habib Bank Limited (HBL) in strengthening trade-related banking links with Bangladesh, China, the Maldives and Sri Lanka to facilitate international transactions and bilateral trade.

The understanding was reached during a meeting between Commerce Minister Jam Kamal Khan and an HBL delegation led by its president and chief executive officer, Muhammad Nassir Salim.

According to an official statement, discussions focused on trade finance, digital banking, export facilitation and improving access to financing for small and medium enterprises (SMEs).

### Strengthening regional banking connections

HBL already operates in all four countries, according to its 2025 annual report.

Its branches in Beijing and Urumqi hold foreign exchange and renminbi licences, supporting transactions involving Chinese businesses.

The proposed cooperation aims to build on HBL's existing international network and improve banking access for Pakistani exporters.

### SME financing and digital banking

Jam Kamal Khan emphasised bringing informal businesses into the documented economy to improve their access to institutional financing.

He also highlighted the importance of international product standards, quality assurance and traceability in strengthening export competitiveness.

HBL representatives discussed challenges in financing undocumented businesses and opportunities to expand digital banking and financial inclusion.

### Expanding export markets

The commerce minister identified trade and investment opportunities in Africa, Southeast Asia and the Middle East, outlining plans for country-specific strategies to diversify exports.

Discussions also covered simplifying regulations, removing overlapping licensing requirements and reducing administrative barriers.

The meeting concluded with an understanding to strengthen coordination between the ministry and HBL on trade finance, investment facilitation and SME development.
