**ISLAMABAD, November 14, 2026 —** Pakistan concluded its two-day Minerals Investment Forum with 13 agreements and memorandums of understanding, alongside announcements of new copper and gold exploration findings in Balochistan.

The agreements involve Pakistani organisations and international partners from Türkiye, Britain, Germany, Japan and other countries. They cover mineral exploration, geological research, laboratory facilities and technologies intended to improve the identification of mineral deposits.

Among the partnerships, Mari Minerals agreed to pursue exploration projects with Türkiye's Lidya Mining and MTA International Company, while a separate arrangement with Ivanhoe Atlantic targets mineral prospects in Chagai district.

Ivanhoe Atlantic said its proposed joint venture with Mari Minerals remains subject to definitive agreements and regulatory approvals.

The Frontier Works Organisation (FWO) also announced an estimated 350 million tonnes of copper in the Dasht-e-Kain area of Chagai, based on analysis of approximately half the available drilling data.

Mari Minerals reported copper and gold findings at East Siah Diq, while National Resources Limited said it had identified mineralisation at eight exploration targets following nearly 25,000 metres of drilling. NRL also announced a $100 million investment commitment.

The forum also introduced geological information platforms intended to improve access to exploration data.

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif called for greater domestic processing of minerals instead of relying on raw-material exports.

Despite the agreements, the sector faces challenges involving security, infrastructure, financing and regulatory approvals. The value of the proposed investments will depend on whether preliminary partnerships advance into funded projects and commercial operations.
