**ISLAMABAD, 19 November 2026 —** The government is developing a **Marble Policy 2026–29** to modernise Pakistan's marble and granite industry, reduce raw-material wastage and improve its competitiveness in international markets.

Adviser to the Prime Minister on Industries and Production Haroon Akhtar Khan outlined the proposed reforms during a meeting with marble industry representatives on Wednesday.

The policy will cover the entire production chain, from quarrying and extraction to processing, polishing and exports, with an emphasis on modern machinery, skilled workers and value-added products.

### Modern Mining and Export Development

Khan said the industry needed scientific mining techniques, improved quality-testing facilities and internationally competitive production standards to make better use of Pakistan's natural stone resources.

The proposed framework will also address transport and infrastructure challenges, including road and rail connections, port facilities and the movement of finished products.

Small and medium-sized businesses are expected to receive greater attention through access to financing, modern equipment and existing export finance schemes.

### Italian Experts and International Exhibition

The government plans to facilitate an upcoming visit by Italian experts, who will assess mining and processing facilities and identify opportunities for technology transfer and investment.

Officials are also considering an international marble exhibition to connect Pakistani producers with foreign buyers and investors.

A stakeholder committee has been directed to submit recommendations for the proposed exhibition within one week.

Khan said consultations on the new marble policy would be completed within **four weeks**, after which the government intends to move towards implementation.

The initiative aims to increase industrial productivity, create employment opportunities and expand Pakistan's exports of processed marble and granite.
