**ISLAMABAD, 19 November 2026 —** Pakistan’s Petroleum Division has finalised a plan to procure 16 liquefied natural gas cargoes over four months to meet winter demand. The government is prioritising long-term contracts as disruptions to Qatari supplies have forced it to seek additional cargoes on the spot market.

Petroleum Minister Ali Pervaiz Malik said at a briefing on Wednesday that November’s requirements were covered. Qatar is due to supply three of the four cargoes needed for December. For January, Pakistan has secured one of the six required shipments under a long-term contract with Eni, while officials are seeking further deliveries from Qatar.

The Petroleum Division is also exploring short-term arrangements with suppliers in Oman, the United States and Azerbaijan’s state oil company, SOCAR. Malik said officials planned to visit Oman to formalise cooperation on LNG and liquefied petroleum gas.

### Pakistan LNG seeks fresh bids

Pakistan LNG Limited is seeking two spot cargoes for delivery at Port Qasim. The December delivery window was moved from 14–16 December to 25–26 December. The January window remains 9–11 January.

The fresh tender follows a November bidding round in which Pakistan rejected offers ranging from $27.10 to $31 per million British thermal units. The Petroleum Division had previously estimated winter requirements at 24 cargoes, including nine for power generation and 15 for domestic consumption. Officials have yet to detail how the revised plan changes that allocation.
