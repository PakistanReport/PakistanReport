The imposition of emergency measures and governor’s rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa has been challenged in the Peshawar High Court, according to reporting published on October 12 and 13.

The petition asks the court to declare the federal action unconstitutional. The president, prime minister and provincial governor have been named as respondents, according to the report.

The dispute concerns the constitutional framework for extraordinary federal intervention in provincial affairs, including Articles 232 and 234. Supporters of the intervention point to security concerns, while critics question the legal basis and implications for elected provincial institutions.

A filed petition is not a judicial finding. The court’s consideration and any eventual ruling must be reported separately. Parliament has also been summoned to consider developments related to the emergency proclamation.
