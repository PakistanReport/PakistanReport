**ISLAMABAD, November 13, 2026 —** Nishat Group has expressed interest in investing in Pakistan's proposed new port following a meeting between its chairman, Mian Muhammad Mansha, and Federal Minister for Maritime Affairs Muhammad Junaid Anwar Chaudhry.

According to a statement issued on Friday, the discussions focused on private investment in port infrastructure and logistics facilities.

Mansha indicated the group's willingness to explore opportunities in the proposed development.

## Government Seeks Private Investment

The maritime affairs minister said greater private-sector participation was needed to expand and modernise Pakistan's ports.

The meeting covered improvements in cargo handling, transport connections and logistics infrastructure to support trade.

Nishat Group, which has interests in textiles, banking, cement and power generation, could potentially expand into maritime infrastructure through the project.

## Why the Project Matters

Pakistan relies heavily on Karachi Port, Port Qasim and Gwadar for international trade.

Additional port capacity and better transport connections could help reduce cargo delays and improve the movement of imports and exports.

The project's development will depend on further planning, financing and private-sector participation.

Nishat Group's interest represents an initial step towards possible involvement in Pakistan's port expansion plans.
