**ISLAMABAD:** Pakistan's listed corporate sector recorded a 25% year-on-year increase in profits during the first nine months of 2026, marking its strongest performance for the period in three years, according to figures highlighted by an adviser to the finance minister.

Khurram Schehzad said corporate earnings had improved across several industries, reflecting stronger business performance and easing financial pressures.

Profits increased 23% during the July–September quarter, the first quarter of fiscal year 2026–27, while non-bank companies reported a 37% increase over the same period.

Engineering companies recorded the largest reported increase, with profits rising 304%. Oil marketing companies followed with growth of 144%, while exploration and production companies posted a 43% rise.

Technology-sector earnings increased 31%, followed by automobiles at 19%, pharmaceuticals at 14%, cement at 13% and fertilisers at 11%.

Schehzad attributed the improvement to greater economic stability, reduced financing pressures and ongoing fiscal and business reforms.

He also pointed to tax relief announced for businesses and exporters in the current federal budget as a measure intended to support investment.

Despite the stronger earnings figures, the adviser said sustained economic progress would depend on companies converting higher profitability into industrial expansion, productive investment and new jobs.
