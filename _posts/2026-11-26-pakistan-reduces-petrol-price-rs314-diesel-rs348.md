**ISLAMABAD:** The federal government has reduced the price of petrol from Rs318 to Rs314 per litre and high-speed diesel from Rs353 to Rs348 per litre, effective November 26.

The latest revision follows another reduction announced on Tuesday and brings diesel prices to their lowest level in four months.

Petroleum prices have fallen steadily since early November, when petrol and high-speed diesel were priced at Rs374.51 and Rs395.20 per litre, respectively.

Under the pricing arrangement in effect on November 25, the petroleum levy stood at Rs80 per litre on both fuels, alongside a climate support levy of Rs5 per litre.

Domestic oil refineries were absorbing Rs17.45 per litre of the petroleum levy on petrol, with consumers paying the remaining Rs62.55. The temporary 30-day arrangement introduced earlier this month is expected to cost refineries a combined Rs15 billion.

International crude oil prices rose during Wednesday's trading session, with Brent crude reaching $93.06 per barrel and West Texas Intermediate trading at $82.97 per barrel.

Domestic petroleum prices are calculated using a seven-working-day rolling average of international petroleum product prices and exchange rates.

Petroleum prices remain substantially below their April 3 peaks, when petrol reached Rs458.41 per litre and high-speed diesel climbed to Rs520.35 following disruptions to global energy supplies caused by the Middle East conflict.

The government has been reviewing petroleum prices on a near-daily basis since July 17, replacing the weekly pricing mechanism introduced in March.
