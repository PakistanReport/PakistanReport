**SWABI:** Construction work at the Tarbela Dam Fifth Extension Hydropower Project has been suspended after a section of rock collapsed during excavation on Monday, allowing water to enter a connecting tunnel and the under-construction powerhouse.

The Water and Power Development Authority (WAPDA) confirmed that the incident occurred on 9 November during excavation of the second bench connecting tunnel at the T-5 project.

Water from an intake pit entered the construction areas after the rock gave way, prompting an emergency evacuation.

WAPDA said all workers were evacuated safely and no deaths or injuries were reported.

## WAPDA Denies Collapse of Main Tunnel

Following reports and videos circulating online, WAPDA clarified that the project's main tunnel had not collapsed.

The authority said the main tunnel, powerhouse structure, tailrace culvert and switchyard had suffered no damage.

According to WAPDA, water had been completely drained from the intake pit, stopping further inflow into the downstream construction areas.

Work to remove water from the under-construction powerhouse had also begun.

Senior WAPDA officials, project engineers and contractors are assessing the situation to determine what repairs or other measures may be required before construction can resume.

## Construction Suspended Pending Assessment

Haripur Deputy Commissioner Waseem Ahmed confirmed that work at the project had been suspended following the incident.

He said an investigation would examine the circumstances surrounding the failure of the protective barrier and determine whether structural or technical problems contributed to the incident.

District authorities also reported that one worker was caught in the water during the emergency but was rescued safely.

No revised construction schedule has been announced.

## Increased Water Releases Prompt Safety Warning

Following the incident, authorities warned residents living near the Indus River that water releases from Tarbela Dam would increase.

The Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Provincial Disaster Management Authority issued an alert to the administrations of Haripur, Swabi and Nowshera, warning that downstream releases could exceed 150,000 cusecs.

Local authorities advised residents to avoid swimming, boating and other activities in the river while water levels remained elevated.

Officials said there was no immediate threat of widespread flooding downstream.

## Project Already Facing Delays and Higher Costs

The Tarbela Fifth Extension Project is designed to add 1,530 megawatts of electricity generation capacity to the existing dam.

Once completed, it is expected to increase Tarbela's total installed capacity from 4,888MW to 6,418MW.

The project has already faced delays following an earlier construction incident involving a protective cofferdam.

A government inquiry into that incident identified problems involving design changes and project supervision.

The project's estimated cost has increased from approximately Rs82 billion to Rs317 billion, while its completion target was previously moved from 2026 to June 2028.

The World Bank and Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank are providing approximately $690 million in financing for the expansion.

It remains unclear whether the latest incident will cause further delays or additional costs. WAPDA has not announced a revised completion date.
