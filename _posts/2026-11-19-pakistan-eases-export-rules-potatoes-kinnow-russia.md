**ISLAMABAD, 19 November 2026 —** Pakistan has granted a one-time exemption from financial instrument requirements for exports of potatoes and kinnow to Russia, allowing eligible shipments through land and sea routes until **May 15, 2027**.

According to an official memorandum reported by Business Recorder, the exemption covers potatoes, kinnow and other mandarins.

### Easier Agricultural Trade

The measure removes the specified financial instrument requirement for eligible exports, easing documentation procedures for traders supplying the Russian market.

Pakistan previously introduced a similar exemption in December 2025 for potato and kinnow exports to Commonwealth of Independent States (CIS) countries through Iran.

In February 2026, Pakistan and Russia agreed to strengthen trade links through expanded land and air connectivity.

Russia also lifted restrictions on potato imports from Punjab in April, reopening access after phytosanitary restrictions imposed in May 2025.

### Growing Potato Production

Pakistan's potato cultivation area has expanded substantially over the past decade, reaching approximately **462,160 hectares in 2025–26**, compared with 177,700 hectares in 2015–16.

Production reached around **12 million tonnes** in the latest season.

The exemption comes ahead of the main kinnow export season, which generally runs from December to April, and could help Pakistani exporters expand agricultural shipments to Russia.
