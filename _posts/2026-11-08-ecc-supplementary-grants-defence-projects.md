# ECC Approves Rs84 Billion in Supplementary Grants for Defence Projects

**ISLAMABAD, November 8, 2026 —** The Economic Coordination Committee (ECC) of the Cabinet has approved three technical supplementary grants totalling approximately Rs84 billion for defence-related requirements, including ongoing projects, an aircraft induction and Project Istaqlal.

The approvals were made at a meeting chaired by Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb on November 4, according to a statement issued by the Finance Division.

## Breakdown of Defence Allocations

The committee approved Rs54 billion for ongoing Defence Services projects and Rs29 billion for Project Istaqlal.

A further Rs1 billion was approved for the induction of a Lineage L-1000 aircraft.

Together, the three allocations amount to approximately Rs84 billion. The Finance Division said they were intended to meet operational and project requirements.

## Other Funding Decisions

The defence grants formed part of a wider set of decisions considered at the meeting, which reviewed 11 agenda items from different ministries and divisions.

The ECC also approved Rs275 billion in supplementary funding for ongoing water-sector projects and Rs1 billion to extend natural gas connections to villages within five kilometres of gas-producing fields.

Separately, it approved approximately Rs4 million for helicopter maintenance requirements of Frontier Corps Balochistan (North).

The committee authorised the transfer of approximately Rs16 billion in savings from austerity and fuel-conservation measures to the Prime Minister's Austerity Fund 2026.

## Additional Policy Approvals

The ECC approved revisions to financing arrangements under the Prime Minister's Youth Business and Agriculture Loan Scheme.

It also permitted continued gas supplies to the FatimaFert and Agritech fertiliser plants until December 31, 2026, subject to supply availability.

Other decisions included extensions and renewals of 89 petroleum exploration and production rights, subject to regulatory conditions, and restrictions on imports of camera-enabled smart glasses.
