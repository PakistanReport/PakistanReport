**KARACHI, 17 November 2026 —** Saudi Arabia's Najd Gateway Holding Company has renewed its bid to acquire an 84.51% controlling stake in Samba Bank Limited, submitting a fresh announcement to the Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX) hours after withdrawing its earlier notice.

The previous announcement was withdrawn after an extended regulatory deadline expired on Tuesday. However, the prospective buyer said it remained committed to the transaction, with negotiations over a share purchase agreement in their final stages.

Arif Habib Limited, acting as manager to the offer, submitted both notices to the PSX. The withdrawal was disclosed at 11:26am, followed by the renewed announcement at 12:10pm.

### Proposed acquisition

Najd Gateway intends to acquire approximately 852 million shares of Samba Bank from The Saudi National Bank, representing 84.51% of the bank's issued capital.

The proposal also includes a public offer for at least 7.75% of the bank's shares, potentially increasing Najd Gateway's total ownership to approximately 92.26%.

Najd Gateway is a privately held Saudi investment company incorporated in January 2024. According to the PSX filing, it is wholly owned by Prince Mansour bin Mohammed S. Al Saud.

### Previous sale attempts

The Saudi National Bank has previously explored selling its controlling stake in Samba Bank.

Bank Alfalah pursued the acquisition in 2024, but the process ended when the Saudi National Bank terminated the proposed sale in November of that year.

Najd Gateway's renewed bid keeps the possibility of a change in Samba Bank's ownership open, although the transaction has not yet been completed.
