**ISLAMABAD:** A delegation of Italian marble specialists is scheduled to visit Pakistan from November 22 to 27 to assess the country's stone industry and identify opportunities for technological cooperation.

According to Pakistan's embassy in Italy, the delegation will be led by Paolo Marone, president of the Italian Marble Institute, accompanied by technical expert Caudia Chiappino.

The experts will visit Marble City in Karachi and engage with the Sindh Economic Zones Management Company before holding meetings with government officials and industry representatives in Islamabad.

Discussions are planned with the Pakistan Mineral Development Corporation and Geological Survey of Pakistan, alongside business meetings and field visits.

Pakistan's Ambassador to Italy, Ali Javed, said cooperation with Italian institutions could help improve stone extraction and finishing, allowing Pakistan to increase exports of higher-value products.

The visit follows discussions involving Italy's foreign ministry, the Italian Trade Agency and marble industry associations in both countries.

Separately, the Ministry of Industries and Production is developing a Marble Policy for 2026–29, aimed at reducing material wastage, modernising mining techniques and improving processing standards.

Adviser to the Prime Minister on Industries and Production Haroon Akhtar Khan has said the proposed policy will address machinery, skills, financing and export competitiveness.

Pakistan has significant reserves of marble, granite, onyx and travertine, while Italy has established expertise in stone processing and finishing.
