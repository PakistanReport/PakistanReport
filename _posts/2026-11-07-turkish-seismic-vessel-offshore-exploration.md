**ISLAMABAD, November 7, 2026 —** A Turkish seismic research vessel is expected to arrive in Karachi between November 17 and 20 as Pakistan prepares to revive offshore oil and gas exploration in cooperation with Türkiye.

The Petroleum Division announced the expected arrival following a high-level meeting chaired by Federal Minister for Petroleum Ali Pervaiz Malik on November 6.

The vessel, operated by Turkish Petroleum Offshore Company (TPOC), is expected to support a new offshore exploration campaign aimed at assessing potential oil and gas resources beneath Pakistan's coastal waters.

## Preparations Underway

The meeting reviewed operational, logistical, maritime and security arrangements for the vessel's arrival and the commencement of exploration activities.

Senior officials from the Petroleum Division, maritime and security authorities, and Pakistan's state-owned energy companies participated in the discussions.

Representatives of Oil and Gas Development Company Limited (OGDCL), Pakistan Petroleum Limited (PPL) and Government Holdings Private Limited (GHPL) were among those present.

The Petroleum Division directed the relevant departments to maintain close coordination to facilitate the vessel's arrival and planned operations.

Minister Ali Pervaiz Malik described the initiative as an important development in Pakistan–Türkiye energy cooperation and assured the Turkish delegation of the government's support.

## Renewed Focus on Offshore Resources

According to the Petroleum Division, the initiative marks an effort to revive Pakistan's offshore exploration activities after nearly two decades.

Seismic surveys collect geological information about formations beneath the seabed, helping exploration companies identify areas that may warrant further investigation or drilling.

The planned campaign forms part of Pakistan's wider efforts to explore domestic energy resources and strengthen long-term energy security.

However, seismic exploration does not establish the existence of commercially recoverable oil or gas reserves. Any potential discoveries would require further assessment and exploratory drilling.

The vessel's arrival remains scheduled for November 17–20, with the start and progress of operations dependent on the necessary arrangements.
