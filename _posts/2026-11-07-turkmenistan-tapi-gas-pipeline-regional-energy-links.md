**ISLAMABAD, November 7, 2026 —** Turkmenistan has reaffirmed the importance of the Turkmenistan–Afghanistan–Pakistan–India (TAPI) gas pipeline and other cross-border infrastructure projects in strengthening energy supplies and economic connectivity between Central and South Asia.

During a briefing for Pakistani media in Islamabad on November 6, the Embassy of Turkmenistan highlighted energy, transport and digital infrastructure as key areas for expanding cooperation with Pakistan.

The embassy said both countries shared an interest in developing reliable energy supplies, improving regional infrastructure and strengthening economic ties.

## TAPI Pipeline Remains Central to Energy Cooperation

The TAPI gas pipeline is intended to transport natural gas from Turkmenistan through Afghanistan to Pakistan and India.

The project has long been identified as a potential source of additional natural gas supplies for Pakistan, which could help diversify the country's energy imports.

The Turkmen embassy described Pakistan as an important partner in expanding access to South Asian energy markets and developing alternative routes for Turkmenistan's gas exports.

## Electricity, Railways and Digital Connectivity

Alongside TAPI, the embassy highlighted the Turkmenistan–Afghanistan–Pakistan (TAP) electricity transmission project as another important component of regional cooperation.

The proposed electricity link is intended to support cross-border power transmission and strengthen energy connections between the participating countries.

The embassy also identified railway development and fibre-optic connectivity as areas offering opportunities for closer economic integration.

These initiatives could support trade, transport and communications between Central Asian and South Asian markets.

## Pakistan's Role in Regional Trade

Turkmenistan said Pakistan's geographical position made it an important partner in developing access to South Asian markets.

The embassy emphasised the potential for stronger cooperation in energy supplies, regional infrastructure and commercial connectivity.

The briefing took place as Turkmenistan marked the 35th anniversary of its independence, with the embassy highlighting the country's continuing focus on international partnerships.

The proposed projects form part of a wider effort to improve regional energy and transport links, although their development remains at different stages.

Pakistan and Turkmenistan continue to identify energy infrastructure and regional connectivity as priorities for bilateral economic cooperation.
