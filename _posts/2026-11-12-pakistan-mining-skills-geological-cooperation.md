**ISLAMABAD:** Pakistan is seeking stronger international cooperation in geological research and mining skills as policymakers, investors and technical specialists gather in Islamabad for the Pakistan Minerals Investment Forum 2026.

The two-day forum opened on Thursday at the Jinnah Convention Centre, bringing together representatives of mining companies, geological organisations, financial institutions and government agencies.

A central focus of the event is improving the technical capabilities needed to identify mineral deposits and develop commercially viable mining projects.

According to the forum's official programme, the Geological Survey of Pakistan is participating in discussions with international geological organisations on research collaboration, data sharing and modern exploration methods.

Representatives of Germany's Federal Institute for Geosciences and Natural Resources, Türkiye's MTA International and the China Geological Survey are among the specialists listed for the geological cooperation session.

The discussions are intended to examine how better geological information and technical partnerships could reduce uncertainty for companies considering investment in mineral exploration.

The forum also includes a dedicated session on developing Pakistan's mining workforce, with industry representatives and training specialists examining the skills needed for future projects.

Greater participation of women in mining is another focus, alongside leadership development and stronger links between employers, universities and vocational institutions.

Pakistan's mineral sector has attracted growing international attention because of its copper, gold and other mineral resources, but converting deposits into operating mines requires specialised technical expertise, financing and infrastructure.

Speaking at the forum's opening, Petroleum Secretary Hameed Yaqoob Sheikh highlighted the government's efforts to improve the investment environment and develop mineral resources responsibly.

The event's technical programme also includes presentations on advanced exploration technology, sustainable mining methods and the commercial development of mineral resources.

Organisers have brought together Pakistani institutions, including the Geological Survey of Pakistan and Pakistan Mineral Development Corporation, with international mining and technology specialists.

The forum continues on Friday, with further discussions on investment conditions, mining infrastructure and the development of Pakistan's mineral industry.
