Karachi Commissioner Syed Hassan Naqvi has imposed restrictions under Section 144 until October 20, citing demonstrations, traffic disruption and security concerns. Reporting on the notification says protests, rallies, sit-ins, processions and other public gatherings involving more than five people are prohibited from October 11 through October 20. Authorities cited blockages at major roads, intersections and roundabouts that impeded public movement and access to hospitals and emergency services. Section 144 allows specified temporary restrictions, but the precise prohibitions, geographic reach and exemptions are governed by the official notification, not by general assumptions about the law. Residents and organisers should consult the commissioner’s order before planning public gatherings. The restriction does not automatically mean all travel, businesses or ordinary activity are prohibited. This report concerns the notified restrictions and does not suggest that every gathering will be treated identically by enforcement authorities. Any extension, withdrawal or court intervention would require an update.
