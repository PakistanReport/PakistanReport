**KARACHI, 19 November 2026 —** The Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX) staged a strong recovery on Thursday, with the benchmark **KSE-100 Index gaining more than 1,300 points** during early trading as investors returned to major stocks.

The index climbed to approximately **177,762 points**, reversing part of the losses recorded over the previous two sessions.

### Buying Returns Across Major Sectors

Buying interest was reported in commercial banks, cement, automobile assemblers, oil and gas exploration, power generation and refineries.

Major companies trading higher included Hub Power Company (HUBCO), Mari Energies, Oil and Gas Development Company (OGDC), Pakistan Petroleum Limited (PPL), Habib Bank Limited (HBL) and United Bank Limited (UBL).

### Recovery Follows Two Days of Losses

The rebound followed a difficult Wednesday session, when the KSE-100 fell **1,223.41 points, or 0.69%**, to close at 176,294.33.

The benchmark had also declined by 665.21 points on Tuesday, as profit-taking and concerns over geopolitical tensions, oil prices and domestic political uncertainty weighed on investor sentiment.

Thursday's early gains indicated renewed buying interest, although the market's final direction remained dependent on trading during the rest of the session.
