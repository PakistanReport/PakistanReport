**KARACHI:** Mobilink Microfinance Bank has signed an agreement to acquire a controlling 82.51% stake in Apna Microfinance Bank, marking a significant proposed transaction in Pakistan's microfinance banking sector.

The agreement involves the purchase of shares held by United Insurance Company of Pakistan and its associated group companies, according to disclosures submitted to the Pakistan Stock Exchange.

The transaction remains subject to corporate and regulatory approvals, including clearance from the State Bank of Pakistan and the Competition Commission of Pakistan.

Apna Microfinance Bank operates more than 70 business locations across Pakistan, with a lending portfolio focused on agriculture, small enterprises and underserved communities.

If completed, the acquisition would combine Apna Bank's established lending network with Mobilink Bank's digital banking capabilities and wider financial services operations.

Mobilink Bank's parent group, VEON, said the agreement supports its strategy of expanding access to financial services in Pakistan.

The proposed acquisition would also strengthen links between Mobilink Bank and the group's broader financial services businesses, including JazzCash and insurance offerings.

Officials said Apna Bank customers would continue to have access to their accounts and existing services during the approval process.

The agreement follows several months of negotiations that began after Mobilink Bank announced its intention to acquire control of Apna Bank in March 2026.
