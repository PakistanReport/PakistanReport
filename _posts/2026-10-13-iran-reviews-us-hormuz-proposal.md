Iran says it is reviewing the United States’ response to its proposal concerning the reopening of the Strait of Hormuz and will reply once that review is complete, Reuters reported on October 12.

The indirect diplomatic exchanges have not produced a publicly confirmed final agreement. The sequence of steps involving shipping through the waterway and wider nuclear-related discussions remains disputed in reporting on the negotiations.

The strait is a crucial route for international oil and gas shipments, and uncertainty over access has implications for energy prices and regional security. The latest exchanges should not be portrayed as a direct face-to-face meeting or as a confirmed reopening deal.

Any change in the status of the waterway, new negotiating format or agreement between the parties will require fresh confirmation from authoritative sources.
