**SAHIWAL:** National Assembly Speaker Sardar Ayaz Sadiq has said elected representatives have the constitutional right to raise public concerns in Parliament, but the assembly floor should not be used to speak against the state, judiciary or armed forces.

Speaking to journalists in Sahiwal on Wednesday, Sadiq said the National Assembly remained committed to democratic debate and meaningful participation by both government and opposition lawmakers.

He rejected the perception that Parliament merely endorses government decisions, describing it as an institution responsible for representing different political viewpoints and strengthening democratic practices.

The speaker said opposition members had been provided additional opportunities to participate in proceedings, with speaking time extended from eight to thirteen hours on important occasions.

He also urged opposition lawmakers to join parliamentary committees, saying their participation was essential to the legislative process.

Addressing political speculation, Sadiq dismissed claims that efforts were underway to remove the government, arguing that political change should take place through constitutional and parliamentary mechanisms.

The remarks come amid continuing debate over the role of Parliament, opposition participation and relations between political representatives and state institutions.
