# PSX Reminds Listed Companies to Disclose Gender Pay Gap Data in Annual Reports

**KARACHI, November 9, 2026 —** The Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX) has reminded listed companies that they must disclose gender pay gap information in their annual reports and on their websites, warning that incomplete or missing disclosures may lead to regulatory action.

The reminder, issued on November 3, specifically addresses companies whose financial years ended on September 30, 2026, as they prepare their forthcoming annual reports.

The requirement originates from Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP) Circular No. 10 of 2024, which introduced mandatory gender pay gap reporting for listed companies.

## Disclosure Requirements

Under the SECP circular, company boards must prepare a statement showing the difference between the remuneration of male and female employees.

The regulator's suggested reporting format requires companies to disclose both the mean and median gender pay gaps.

The mean figure compares the average hourly pay of male and female employees, while the median calculation compares the midpoint of hourly pay across the two groups.

The calculations cover eligible employees receiving their full basic pay during the relevant period, with specified allowances and leave payments included.

Overtime payments, pension contributions, termination payments and certain non-cash benefits are excluded under the calculation guidance.

The statement is to be signed by the chief executive officer or a director on behalf of the company's board.

## Existing Rules Reinforced

SECP introduced the disclosure requirement in April 2024 under Section 510 of the Companies Act, 2017.

The rules apply to listed companies with financial years ending on or after June 30, 2024.

PSX has subsequently issued reminders to encourage compliance, including notices in 2024 and October 2026.

The latest notice places particular emphasis on companies with September financial year-ends, requiring them to include the information in their upcoming reports and make it available on their websites.

## Regulatory Compliance

SECP previously expressed concern over limited compliance with the reporting requirement despite earlier guidance and awareness efforts.

The regulator has warned that failure to provide the required information, or publishing incomplete disclosures, may result in enforcement action.

The disclosure framework forms part of the Prime Minister's Women Empowerment Package 2024, which includes measures intended to improve gender equality and transparency in the corporate sector.

The November reminder reinforces an existing reporting obligation rather than introducing a new disclosure regime.
