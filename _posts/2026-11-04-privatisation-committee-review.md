**Islamabad, November 4, 2026** — The National Assembly's Standing Committee on Privatisation scheduled its 17th meeting for November 4 at Parliament House, amid continuing parliamentary scrutiny of state-owned enterprises.

The session was scheduled for 11:30 a.m. in Committee Room No. 7.

At its previous meeting on September 17, the committee reviewed developments involving Pakistan International Airlines (PIA), electricity distribution companies and Postal Life Insurance.

### PIA and Power Companies

Officials informed the committee in September that an Arif Habib-led consortium had submitted a winning bid of **Rs135 billion for a 75% stake in PIA**.

Management control was transferred in June 2026, while the buyer expressed interest in acquiring the remaining 25%, subject to contractual requirements.

The committee was also briefed on plans to privatise electricity distribution companies through the sale of 51% to 100% shareholdings with management control.

Ten parties had been prequalified for Faisalabad Electric Supply Company, while 11 submissions for Gujranwala Electric Power Company were under evaluation.

### Outstanding Insurance Claims

The September briefing also identified **Rs15.5 billion in outstanding Postal Life Insurance liabilities**, covering approximately 55,450 claims as of June 2026.

The committee called for additional funding to address delayed payments and emphasised transparency and regulatory oversight in privatisation transactions.
