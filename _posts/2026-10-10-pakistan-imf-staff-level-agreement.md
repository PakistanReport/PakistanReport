Pakistan and the International Monetary Fund have reached a staff-level agreement on reviews of two lending programmes that could make approximately $1.21 billion available after approval by the IMF Executive Board.

The agreement covers the fourth review of Pakistan's $7 billion Extended Fund Facility and the third review of the Resilience and Sustainability Facility.

The prospective financing comprises about $1 billion under the EFF and $210 million under the RSF. These are potential disbursements, not funds already received.

### What the agreement means

An IMF staff-level agreement indicates that the Fund's negotiating team and Pakistani authorities have reached a common position on the programme review.

The IMF Executive Board must still consider and approve the reviews before the associated financing can be released.

The distinction matters because announcements of staff-level agreements are sometimes mistaken for immediate payments.

### Policy priorities

The IMF has continued to emphasise fiscal discipline, stronger tax administration and measures to reduce financial losses in the energy sector.

It has also called for broad fuel support to be phased out promptly, with any future assistance targeted at vulnerable households, temporary and covered within the budget.

Pakistan faces a difficult balance between protecting consumers from high energy costs and limiting spending that could add to fiscal pressure.

The climate-focused RSF supports a separate set of reforms intended to improve resilience to environmental and economic shocks.

### What happens next

Board approval is the next formal step in the financing process. Pakistan's implementation of agreed policies will remain important during subsequent reviews.

For the economy, IMF support can help manage external financing needs, but it does not eliminate risks from higher imported energy costs, debt repayments or changes in global financial conditions.

The agreement therefore marks progress in Pakistan's engagement with the lender, rather than the completion of the programme.
