Pakistan and the International Monetary Fund have reached a staff-level agreement on reviews of two lending programmes, potentially making about $1.21 billion available after approval by the IMF Executive Board.

The agreement covers the fourth review under the $7 billion Extended Fund Facility and the third under the $1.4 billion Resilience and Sustainability Facility. The prospective amount comprises approximately $1 billion under the EFF and $210 million under the RSF.

The IMF also called for prompt phasing-out of broad fuel support and said any future assistance should be targeted, temporary and accommodated within the budget. A staff-level agreement is not a disbursement, and the funds have not been released solely because of this announcement.
