**ISLAMABAD, 20 November 2026 —** American companies represented at a business gathering in Pakistan reported combined revenue of approximately **Rs. 956 billion**, highlighting their contribution to investment, employment and economic activity in the country.

American Business Council of Pakistan President Adnan Asad shared the figures during an event attended by Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif on Thursday.

### Investment and Employment Contributions

According to Asad, the companies represented at the gathering invested more than **Rs. 74 billion over the past five years** and reinvested another **Rs. 32 billion during the past two years**.

Their operations directly employed approximately **96,000 people**, while supporting nearly two million jobs indirectly.

The participating businesses also reported exports worth around **$123 million** and contributions of approximately **Rs. 185 billion** to the national exchequer.

Asad said around 80 American companies were represented at the gathering, including 60 council members and 20 businesses seeking membership.

### Government Pledges to Improve Investment Climate

Addressing the gathering, Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif reaffirmed the government's commitment to export-led economic growth and greater cooperation with American investors.

He said the government would work to address legitimate investor concerns, including bureaucratic delays and procedural barriers affecting business activity.

The prime minister also highlighted opportunities to expand bilateral trade, including Pakistani exports of surgical instruments, sports goods and other manufactured products to the United States.
