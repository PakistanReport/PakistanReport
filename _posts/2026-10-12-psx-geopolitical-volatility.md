Pakistan’s equity market entered the week of October 12 after a volatile period in which geopolitical tensions outweighed some optimism around the country’s engagement with the International Monetary Fund. During the preceding trading week, the benchmark KSE-100 index lost 1,066 points, or about 0.6%, closing at approximately 167,089 points, according to brokerage figures reported by Dawn. Investors also assessed an IMF staff-level agreement that could unlock about $1.2 billion, subject to the Fund’s Executive Board approval.
