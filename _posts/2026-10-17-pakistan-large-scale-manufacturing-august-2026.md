Pakistan's large-scale manufacturing output increased 1.85% during July–August of fiscal year 2026–27 compared with the same period a year earlier, according to provisional Pakistan Bureau of Statistics data.

## August figures

The Quantum Index of Manufacturing stood at 113.19 in August 2026. Output increased 0.67% from August 2025 but declined 4.96% from July 2026. The two comparisons measure different periods and should not be confused.

## Uneven sector performance

The official July–August figures show declines of 2.98% in textiles, 10.78% in chemicals, 6.47% in pharmaceuticals, 11.48% in iron and steel products, and 8.86% in food manufacturing.

These declines demonstrate why a positive headline index does not mean every industry is recovering. The data do not establish the causes of each sector's weakness, which may reflect different demand, cost and supply conditions.

## What to watch

The manufacturing figures are provisional and can be revised. Future releases will indicate whether growth broadens across industries or remains uneven.
