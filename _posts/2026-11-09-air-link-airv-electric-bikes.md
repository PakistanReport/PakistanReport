**LAHORE, November 9, 2026 —** Air Link Communication Limited has entered Pakistan's electric two-wheeler market with the launch of its AirV brand, introducing 11 electric motorcycle and scooter models across six product series.

The company announced the launch in Lahore on November 4 through a material information disclosure submitted to the Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX).

The new business marks a diversification for Air Link, which is known for its mobile phone distribution and manufacturing operations.

## Eleven Models Across Six Series

According to the company's stock exchange disclosure, the AirV range includes 11 models designed for different customer requirements.

Air Link said selected models offer riding ranges of up to 200 kilometres, depending on the variant.

The company also highlighted features including cruise control, regenerative battery technology, near-field communication (NFC) functionality and anti-theft systems.

The stated range represents the company's product specification and may vary according to the model and operating conditions.

## Manufacturing Plans in Lahore

The AirV project is being implemented at Air Link's existing manufacturing facility in the Sundar Green Special Economic Zone in Lahore.

The company's board approved its entry into electric bike manufacturing in July 2026.

Air Link intends to use its manufacturing infrastructure and nationwide distribution network to expand its presence in the electric mobility sector.

The company has also outlined plans to increase local manufacturing content over time, with a long-term localisation target of up to 95%.

Earlier information shared with investors indicated that the initial rollout would involve electric bikes sourced from international partners before a gradual transition towards local assembly and production.

## Expansion Beyond Mobile Phones

Air Link's move into electric vehicles adds a new product category to its existing consumer technology operations.

The company said it aims to develop a scalable electric mobility business serving both individual riders and enterprise customers.

The launch comes as electric two-wheelers attract interest in Pakistan as an alternative to conventional petrol-powered motorcycles.

Air Link's November stock exchange disclosure did not provide a complete price list for all 11 models.

The company's expansion plans will depend on the development of its manufacturing operations, distribution arrangements and customer demand.
