**BERLIN, 19 November 2026 —** European leaders are gathering in Berlin on Thursday to discuss security guarantees for Ukraine and coordinate their position on efforts to end Russia's war, as the continent seeks a greater role in US-led peace negotiations.

German Chancellor Friedrich Merz is hosting the meeting of the *Coalition of the Willing* alongside French President Emmanuel Macron and British Prime Minister Andy Burnham. Leaders from Poland, Italy and other European countries are expected to participate, along with Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy.

The discussions will focus on continued military and financial assistance for Kyiv, security arrangements following a possible ceasefire and preparations for further diplomatic talks.

### Europe Seeks Role in Peace Negotiations

The Berlin meeting comes as US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner prepare to hold consultations with Ukrainian and European officials.

The American representatives are working on proposals covering post-war security guarantees, Ukraine's economic recovery and the next round of negotiations involving Russia and Ukraine.

European governments have pressed for direct involvement in the diplomatic process, arguing that any settlement would have major consequences for the continent's security.

A negotiating group involving Germany, France, Britain, Poland and Italy has emerged as European leaders seek a coordinated position.

However, Russia has not formally agreed to European participation in future negotiations, leaving the proposed diplomatic arrangements uncertain.

### Security Guarantees and Military Support

The Coalition of the Willing, which includes more than 30 countries, has been examining how its members could support Ukraine after a potential ceasefire.

Possible measures include monitoring a ceasefire and providing security commitments intended to prevent renewed fighting.

The Berlin discussions are also expected to address Ukraine's immediate military requirements as Russian attacks on energy infrastructure increase pressure on the country ahead of winter.

Britain is preparing additional drone assistance, while other European governments have announced further support for air defence and military equipment.

### Ukraine Faces Major Funding Needs

Financial assistance is another central issue as European governments consider how to sustain Ukraine's defence and public services.

On Wednesday, the European Commission proposed a **€45 billion loan for 2027**, including funding for military requirements and government expenditure.

The proposal comes amid estimates that Ukraine could face a substantial financing gap next year, even with additional European support.

The Berlin summit is taking place alongside diplomatic meetings involving US President Donald Trump, Russian President Vladimir Putin and Chinese President Xi Jinping in China.

European leaders are seeking to ensure that decisions about Ukraine's future are made with the participation of Kyiv and its European partners.
