The federal government has authorised Pakistan Army troops and assets to assist civil authorities in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa following the declaration of emergency and governor’s rule. The authorisation was reported on October 11 alongside the wider constitutional measures. Officials described the purpose as supporting security and protecting public safety and government installations. An authorisation to deploy is not, by itself, proof that troops are present in every district or that any particular operation has begun. Details about locations, units and operational outcomes should be reported only when independently confirmed or attributed to an official statement. The decision has political as well as security implications because it follows the federal government’s intervention in the PTI-led province. The government cites militant threats, while opposition figures dispute the political and constitutional basis of the emergency.
