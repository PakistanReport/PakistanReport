The Pakistan Meteorological Department has forecast intermittent rain, wind and thunderstorms in parts of upper and central Pakistan between October 10 and 15, with isolated hailstorms possible.

The forecast is linked to a westerly weather system affecting upper regions. Areas covered include parts of KP, Punjab, Islamabad, Kashmir and Gilgit-Baltistan, with different timings and conditions across districts.

The PMD’s daily outlook also indicates that many places may remain dry on individual days. The advisory does not predict continuous rainfall across the country. Residents and farmers should consult updated district forecasts, particularly where gusty winds or hail could affect crops and travel.
