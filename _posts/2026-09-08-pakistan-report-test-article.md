# Pakistan Report Publishing System Test

This is a test article for the Pakistan Report pilot.

The purpose of this article is to confirm that our new publishing system can automatically create an article page from a simple Markdown file.

## Why this matters

Once the system is working, new articles will no longer require us to manually redesign the homepage.

We will simply create a new article, add its headline, category, image and text, and the website will generate the appropriate article page.

## Pakistan Report

Pakistan Report is an independent digital publication focused on Pakistan, with particular attention to economy, business, jobs, technology, explainers and important national developments.
