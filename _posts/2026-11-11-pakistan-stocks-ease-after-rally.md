**KARACHI:** The Pakistan Stock Exchange's benchmark KSE-100 index declined by 251.44 points on Wednesday, closing at 179,083.80 after a sharp rally in the previous session.

The index fell 0.14% during the day, having reached an intraday high of 180,769.22 and a low of 178,642.36.

On Tuesday, the KSE-100 had surged 5,448.56 points, or 3.13%, to 179,335.24 as investors returned following the Iqbal Day holiday.

Market reports linked Tuesday's gains to strong remittance inflows, lower international oil prices and easing concerns over regional tensions.

Wednesday's ready-market trading volume increased to 1.248 billion shares from 972.43 million in the previous session.

However, the value of shares traded declined to Rs41.81 billion from Rs50.67 billion.

Of the 498 companies traded in the ready market, 255 advanced, 215 declined and 28 remained unchanged.

Total market capitalisation fell to Rs19.377 trillion from Rs19.397 trillion on Tuesday.
