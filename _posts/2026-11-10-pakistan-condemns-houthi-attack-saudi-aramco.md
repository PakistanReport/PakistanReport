**ISLAMABAD:** Pakistan has condemned an attempted Houthi missile attack on a Saudi Aramco administrative building in Riyadh, calling for an immediate end to attacks targeting Saudi Arabia.

In a statement issued on Monday, 9 November, the Foreign Office expressed concern over the incident and reaffirmed Pakistan's support for the kingdom's security and territorial integrity.

The condemnation followed an attack on Sunday, 8 November, when Saudi air defence systems intercepted missiles launched towards Riyadh. Falling debris reportedly caused limited damage to an Aramco building in the King Abdullah Financial District. Saudi authorities said no casualties were reported.

## Pakistan Calls for End to Attacks

The Foreign Office urged the Houthis to stop attacks against Saudi Arabia, warning that targeting civilian facilities threatens lives and regional stability.

Pakistan also expressed solidarity with the Saudi leadership and people, while acknowledging the response of Saudi authorities to the incident.

The Houthis claimed responsibility for the attempted strike, which came amid renewed regional tensions.

## Concerns Over Regional Security

Saudi Aramco is one of the world's largest energy companies, and the security of its facilities is closely watched because of Saudi Arabia's importance to global oil markets.

Although the reported damage was limited, the incident highlighted the risks posed by missile and drone attacks on civilian and commercial infrastructure.

Pakistan maintains close diplomatic, economic and defence ties with Saudi Arabia and has consistently supported efforts to protect the kingdom from such attacks.
