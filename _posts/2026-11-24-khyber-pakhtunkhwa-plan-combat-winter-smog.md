**PESHAWAR:** The Khyber Pakhtunkhwa government has prepared a province-wide action plan to combat winter smog, targeting industrial and vehicular emissions, agricultural waste burning and construction-related pollution while introducing measures to protect public health.

According to the provincial Environment Department, authorities will monitor the Air Quality Index (AQI) daily and issue advance warnings when pollution reaches dangerous levels.

The government is considering imposing Section 144 during severe smog episodes to prohibit the burning of garbage and crop residues, with legal action proposed against violators.

Factories and brick kilns will face stricter enforcement of environmental emission standards, while vehicles producing excessive smoke could be fined or impounded.

The plan also includes dust-control measures at construction sites, improved solid waste management and increased road cleaning and water sprinkling.

Educational institutions may face temporary closures or revised school timings during periods of severe air pollution, alongside restrictions on outdoor activities for children.

Hospitals will be placed on heightened alert and instructed to maintain adequate supplies of essential medicines and oxygen. Rescue 1122 personnel will also remain on standby along major highways and motorways.

The government has proposed establishing a Smog Control Committee chaired by the provincial chief secretary, with district-level committees coordinating enforcement and deputy commissioners submitting fortnightly progress reports.

The measures follow an interdepartmental meeting chaired by Climate Change, Forestry, Environment and Wildlife Secretary Junaid Khan, during which responsibilities were assigned to relevant government departments.

The provincial Information Department will coordinate public awareness campaigns targeting schools, commercial centres and residential communities, while the Provincial Disaster Management Authority will obtain smog and fog forecasts to support emergency preparedness.
